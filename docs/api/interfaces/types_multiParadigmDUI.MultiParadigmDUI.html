      Ecological Harmony (Sustainability-Focused)

      Data: Richardson et al. 2023 (9 planetary boundaries), Global Footprint Network 2024 (188 countries) Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH (±50% uncertainty on some boundaries) Drives: Boundary transgression → crises Reports: 9 boundaries, ecological footprint, climate stability, air quality

      Utopia: All 9 boundaries safe, footprint ≤1.5 gha (ZERO countries currently) Dystopia: 6+ boundaries breached (current global state)
