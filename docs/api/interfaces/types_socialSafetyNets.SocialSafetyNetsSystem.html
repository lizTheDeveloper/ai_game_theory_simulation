    Social Safety Nets & Community Infrastructure System (TIER 2.2)

    Physical and social infrastructure to combat loneliness epidemic and enable community flourishing. Based on WHO (2025), Harvard Making Caring Common (2024), US Surgeon General (2024), University of Texas (2025), World Bank (2025).

    Key Finding: "Three-quarters of those surveyed said they wanted more opportunities to develop meaningful relationships. Public and private leaders must build up social infrastructure to help people develop meaningful connections." - Harvard (Oct 2024)
