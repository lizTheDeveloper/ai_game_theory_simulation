# Critical Paths

## Utopia (known)

- 6 upward spirals active for 12+ months
- sustainability >= 65%
- no active crises
- QoL metrics above thresholds

## Defensive AI Deployment (known)

- AI capability > threshold
- misaligned AI detected
- government approval
- adequate funding

## Extinction (known)

- population < 10K
- OR mortality > 99.99%
- OR instant kill (grey goo)
- OR slow collapse (24-120 months)

## INFERRED PATHS

### Dystopia Paths
- High control + low trust → Control Dystopia
- High automation + meaning crisis → Social Collapse
- Fossil industry capture + climate crisis → Environmental Collapse

### Crisis Cascades
- Famine → Migration → Conflict → Nuclear Risk
- Climate → Wet-bulb → Habitability Loss → Migration
- Ocean Acidification → Fishery Collapse → Famine
- Phosphorus Depletion → Agricultural Collapse → Famine

### Upward Spirals (Utopia Path)
- Abundance: Prosperity → Trust → Stability → More Prosperity
- Cognitive: Trust → Risk-taking → Breakthroughs → More Trust
- Democratic: Trust + Institutions → AI Rights → Legitimacy
- Scientific: Research → Breakthroughs → Capabilities → More Research
- Meaning: Purpose → Social Flourishing → Stability
- Ecological: Tech → Environmental Recovery → Sustainability

### Technology Unlock Paths
- AI Capability > 2.0 → Advanced research breakthroughs
- Fusion Materials + Plasma Control → Fusion Energy
- Scalable Oversight + Mechanistic Interp → Alignment Confidence
- Habitat Restoration → Biosphere Recovery (25% → 100%)
