=== EXECUTIVE SUMMARY ===

Ocean acidification = Earth's FIRST crossed climate tipping point (2024-2025).
Tipping point crossed at 1.2-1.4°C warming. Even Paris 1.5°C → >99% coral loss.
Recovery requires not just stabilization but COOLING planet to ~1°C above pre-industrial.

=== KEY FINDINGS ===

pH THRESHOLDS (high confidence):
- Current: 7.9 (30% more acidic than pre-industrial 8.1)
- Stress: pH < 7.9, aragonite Ω < 3.0
- Severe: pH < 7.8, Ω < 2.5 (pteropod dissolution, synergistic bleaching)
- Collapse: pH < 7.7, Ω < 2.0 (calcification ceases)
- 2100 range: 7.68 (RCP8.5) to 8.06 (RCP1.9)

CORAL LOSS TIMELINE:
- 2024-2025: Tipping point crossed (Nature 2025)
- 2050: 70-90% loss under RCP8.5 (IPCC AR6)
- 2100: >99% loss even at 1.5°C warming (very high confidence)

HUMAN IMPACT:
- Population at risk: 500M-1B people (130M in Coral Triangle alone)
- Protein dependence: 50-60% in Pacific Islands, 30-50% SE Asia coastal
- Economic value: $9.9-11T/year ecosystem services
  - Fisheries: $6.8B/year
  - Tourism: $19.5B/year (Asia-Pacific)
  - Coastal protection: $80B+/year avoided damages

CASCADE MECHANISM:
1. Coral bleaching (pH < 7.9) → 70-90% coral loss by 2050
2. Fisheries collapse (5-15 year lag) → 50-90% fish biomass decline
3. Food insecurity (immediate) → 500M-1B lose primary protein source

COMPOUND STRESSORS:
- Warming + acidification = SYNERGISTIC (more than additive)
- 31°C + pH 7.8 → severe bleaching within 5 days
- Overfishing removes herbivores → algal overgrowth → prevents recovery

REVERSIBILITY:
- Chemical changes "irreversible on centennial to millennial timescales"
- Lab experiments: pH restoration CAN increase calcification (Albright 2016)
- Reality: Ocean-basin scale reversal requires massive carbon removal
- Recovery timescale: 50-200 years IF pH returns to 8.1 + cooling to 1°C

OCEAN ALKALINIZATION (potential tech):
- Lab/mesocosm: Proven to increase calcification
- Great Barrier Reef: Could offset acidification by ~10 years
- Current status: Lab scale only
- 2030-2050 potential: Small-scale (protected lagoons, 0.1-1 km²)
- Large-scale: Unknown feasibility, ecological safety unvalidated
