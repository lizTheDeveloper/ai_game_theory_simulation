## Key Research Findings

### First Tipping Point Crossed (2024-2025)

**Status:** Warm-water coral reefs crossed thermal tipping point at ~1.4°C warming

**Threshold:** ~1.2°C above pre-industrial
**Current:** ~1.4°C (2024 average)
**Implication:** Irreversible decline even if temperatures stabilize

### AMOC Collapse Risk

**Timeline:** "Could occur within lifetimes of people born today"
**Threshold:** <2°C global warming
**Current State:** Weakest in at least 1,000 years
**Regional Impacts:**
- Europe: 5-15°C cooling (despite global warming)
- Amazon: Severe drought → forest dieback
- Sea level: +50 cm along US East Coast

### Rate-Induced Tipping (NEW MECHANISM)

**Discovery:** Fast Greenland ice melt can trigger AMOC collapse even without crossing AMOC's intrinsic threshold

**Mechanism:**
- Speed of change matters independently of magnitude
- Abrupt ice shelf collapses (days-months) → freshwater pulse
- Pulse rate exceeds AMOC adjustment capacity → collapse

**Source:** van Westen et al. (2024), Earth System Dynamics

### Tipping Cascade Pathways

```
GIS fast melt → AMOC collapse → Amazon drought → Antarctic warming
                            ↓
                    Southern Hemisphere impacts
```

### Ice Sheet Uncertainty is Decisive

**Key Finding:** "Neglecting polar ice sheets can alter expected tipped element count by >2×"

**Source:** Communications Earth & Environment (2024)

**Implication:** Ice sheet dynamics are single largest uncertainty in cascade modeling

## Simulation Implications

Must model:

1. **Non-linear tipping dynamics**
   - Discontinuous jumps, not gradual degradation
   - Threshold-based state transitions

2. **Hysteresis**
   - Systems don't recover when cooling below threshold
   - Recovery threshold << tipping threshold (e.g., 0.8°C vs 1.2°C)

3. **Stochastic timing**
   - Probability distributions, not fixed thresholds
   - Each 0.5°C increment roughly doubles cascade risk

4. **Cascade propagation**
   - Tipping in one system increases probability in others
   - Interaction matrix: GIS → AMOC (0.3), AMOC → Amazon (0.5)

5. **Rate-induced tipping**
   - Fast changes trigger tipping independently
   - Must track dT/dt, not just T
