FINDINGS FROM TEST RUN
-----------------------
Hypothesis: If research budget = $100B/month (2x threshold), scientific spiral
should activate by month 6 (assumes workflow adaptation follows).

RESULT: ❌ Scientific spiral DID NOT activate
- Research budget: $10B → $100B ✅ (applied correctly)
- Tech deployed: 73/73 at 100% ✅
- Workflow adaptation: 0% ❌ (BLOCKER: need 25%)

CRITICAL INSIGHT:
Even with $100B/month research (10x default, 2x threshold), scientific spiral
failed because organizations need TIME to adapt workflows. Instant tech deployment
in god mode doesn't give organizations the months/years needed to restructure.

This reveals a fundamental constraint:
- Technology can be deployed instantly (god mode assumption)
- Research can be funded instantly (scenario override)
- But organizational adaptation has INHERENT TIME DELAYS (S-curve adoption)

Implication: Scientific spiral requires EITHER:
1. Longer simulation runs (allow workflow adaptation to reach 25% naturally), OR
2. Starting condition override (workflowAdaptation: 0.25 at month 0), OR
3. Different scenario (test without god mode, let tech deploy gradually)

This is EXACTLY the kind of insight scenario testing was designed to reveal.
