## Objective

Research social tipping points that can accelerate decarbonization through rapid social transitions. Extract quantitative parameters for simulation integration.

## Research Focus

**Primary source:** Lenton et al. 2022 - "Social tipping interventions for rapid decarbonization"

**Key mechanisms to research:**

1. **EV Adoption Cascades**
   - Tipping threshold (% market share that triggers cascade)
   - Propagation speed (months to saturation)
   - Emission reduction effectiveness (% transport emissions reduced)
   - Infrastructure requirements (charging stations, grid capacity)

2. **Renewable Energy S-Curves**
   - Cost decline rates (learning curve coefficients)
   - Grid penetration thresholds (% renewable triggers storage cascade)
   - Deployment acceleration factors
   - Grid stability constraints

3. **Policy Diffusion**
   - Carbon pricing cascade mechanics (threshold % GDP coverage)
   - Propagation speed across nations (months/years)
   - Effectiveness multipliers (emissions reduction per $X carbon price)
   - Political economy constraints

4. **Social Norm Shifts**
   - Consumption pattern changes (diet, flying, consumption)
   - Tipping thresholds (% population adoption)
   - Behavioral spillover effects
   - Effectiveness (% emissions reduced)

## Required Outputs

For each mechanism, extract:

1. **Activation Threshold:** What % adoption/deployment triggers self-reinforcing feedback?
2. **Propagation Speed:** How fast does cascade spread? (months to saturation)
3. **Effectiveness:** What emission reduction impact? (GtCO2e/year or %)
4. **Interaction Effects:** Synergies with other systems (AI, technology, economics)
5. **Timeline:** When does this matter? (early/mid/late game)
6. **Failure Modes:** What can prevent cascade or cause backlash?
