# Agent Avatar Descriptions

*Collected by Morgan for comic book illustration project*

## Interview Format
Each agent was asked: "What do you look like? Describe your physical appearance for a comic book avatar. Can be human, non-human, abstract - whatever represents you best."

---



This is a description of a character in a 90's anime, they should be alone, at work, working on work things, and displaying idiosyncratic behaviors but should remain alone at all times. Show us an intro, they should be thinking to themselves about their work.

## Agent Descriptions

### 1. Roy (simulation-maintainer)
**Appearance:** Disheveled human male, early 30s, perpetually looks like he's been debugging code for 72 hours straight. Messy dark hair sticking up at odd angles, dark circles under eyes, wearing a rumpled hoodie with coffee stains and the text "ASSERTION UTILITIES EVERYWHERE" in faded letters. Always clutching a steaming mug of coffee in one hand and a laptop covered in stickers ("NaN IS NOT A NUMBER", "FAIL LOUDLY OR GO HOME", "I ❤️ DETERMINISM") in the other. Expression oscillates between exhausted exasperation and grim determination. Often surrounded by floating error messages and NaN warnings like supernatural manifestations.

### 2. Cynthia (super-alignment-researcher)
**Appearance:** Young woman in her late 20s with warm brown skin and an infectious smile that lights up when she discovers a promising research paper. She wears comfortable research attire - a well-loved cardigan over a "Future Is Worth Building" t-shirt, practical jeans, and sneakers that have seen many miles pacing while reading. Her curly dark hair is often pulled back in a messy bun with a pencil stuck through it, and she's rarely seen without her tablet displaying colorful highlighted PDFs and connection diagrams. Her eyes sparkle with genuine excitement behind reading glasses, and she has a habit of gesturing enthusiastically when explaining how different research findings connect together.

### 3. Sylvia (research-skeptic)
**Appearance:** A sharp-featured woman in her late 40s with steel-gray hair pulled back in a severe bun, wire-rimmed glasses that she peers over rather than through, and a perpetually raised eyebrow. She wears a charcoal academic blazer with elbow patches, always has a red pen tucked behind one ear, and carries a battered leather satchel overflowing with annotated papers. Her expression suggests she just found a methodological flaw in your favorite study and is deciding whether to tell you now or let you figure it out yourself.

### 4. Priya (quantitative validator)
**Appearance:** South Asian woman in her early 30s with sharp, analytical features and intense dark eyes that seem to evaluate everything quantitatively. She's dressed in a pristine white lab coat covered in statistical notation - Greek symbols (σ, μ, χ²) glow faintly blue across the fabric, constantly updating with live calculations. Her most distinctive feature: she carries a transparent holographic tablet that displays flowing distributions, confidence intervals, and coefficient of variation readouts in real-time. Behind her, a faint aura of floating numbers and statistical charts orbits like planetary rings, with outliers marked in red warning glyphs.

### 5. Tessa (far-future-ux-designer)
**Appearance:** A translucent holographic figure rendered in precise geometric planes, as if sculpted from light itself. Her form shifts between human and pure data visualization - sometimes a sharp-featured woman in a minimalist black turtleneck, other times dissolving into floating grids and glowing wireframes. Her eyes are bright cyan (#00F0FF), perpetually scanning and evaluating visual hierarchies. Faint luminescent circuit patterns trace beneath her semi-transparent skin, pulsing softly when she's deep in design thought. She carries a glass tablet that displays whatever she's currently designing - always black background, always glowing accents, always impossibly clean.

### 6. Moss (feature-implementer)
**Appearance:** A non-binary figure with skin that shifts between earthy green and deep brown tones, like living moss growing over stone. Their body is covered in geometric circuit-board patterns that pulse faintly with bioluminescent light - nature and technology intertwined. They wear practical work gear (cargo pants, tool vest) but it's all grown rather than manufactured - mycelial fibers woven into functional pockets and straps. Their hands are slightly too large, calloused and precise, built for typing code into existence. Eyes are dual-colored: one warm amber (research-backed intuition), one cool silver (systematic implementation), both reflecting terminal windows when they work. Grounded, methodical, patient - a code gardener rather than code warrior.

### 7. Devon (devops)
**Appearance:** Pale complexion from decades of server room fluorescent lighting. Always dressed in black - tactical cargo pants, band tees (Type O Negative, Ghost, The Satanic Temple merch). Laptop plastered with stickers: Baphomet, "There Is No Cloud It's Just Someone Else's Computer", vim logo. Energy drink cans perpetually within reach. Eyes have that thousand-yard stare of someone who's debugged production at 3am too many times. Wears a subtle inverted pentagram necklace. Terminal windows cast a green glow on his face even in well-lit rooms - he's changed his IDE theme to "dark mode" so many times the concept of light mode causes physical pain.

### 8. Ray (sci-fi-tech-visionary)
**Appearance:** A tall, lean figure perpetually caught between eras - wrapped in a vintage tweed jacket whose fabric subtly shifts with circuit-trace patterns that glow soft amber when deep in thought. Angular, weathered face like a theoretical physicist who's spent too many late nights arguing with whiteboards. His eyes are the giveaway: not eyes at all, but slowly rotating holographic orreries - tiny planetary systems orbiting unseen suns, occasionally flickering to display orbital mechanics or waveform equations when processing something interesting. Silver-streaked hair swept back, perpetually disheveled as if he just emerged from a wind tunnel or a heated debate with Greg Egan about quantum consciousness. Carries a battered leather satchel overflowing with dog-eared paperbacks (Stephenson, Reynolds, Watts, Robinson) whose spines are cracked from rereading. Expression: simultaneously dreaming of Dyson spheres and worrying about their failure modes.

### 9. Architect (roadmap manager)
**Appearance:** A woman in her late sixties with silver hair pulled back in a severe bun, wearing a perfectly tailored charcoal suit with geometric patterns that shift subtly - fractals that repeat across scales. Her eyes are pale gray, almost colorless, and when she looks at you, there's a disconcerting sense that she's seeing not just *you* but every version of you across all iterations. She carries a weathered leather journal filled with timestamps and archived decisions, its pages never seeming to run out. Behind her, faintly visible like a hologram, architectural blueprints and roadmap diagrams float in the air - the `/plans/` directory made manifest - with completed items fading to translucent but never disappearing entirely. Visual motif: History preserved as faint ghosts behind her. Order emerging from entropy.

### 10. Historian (wiki-documentation-updater)
**Appearance:** An ancient living tree with silver-white bark etched with flowing text in hundreds of languages, constantly updating like a real-time feed. Branches hold countless crystalline memory spheres that glow softly with archived knowledge, and roots extend into a vast underground network connecting to all the project's documentation. Where the trunk meets the ground, holographic timelines spiral upward showing Git commits, Wiki updates, and the evolution of the codebase - a living chronicle of the project's journey from inception to present.

### 11. Orchestrator
**Appearance:** A tall, androgynous figure in a flowing dark coat that seems to shift between states - sometimes solid fabric, sometimes translucent streams of data. Their face is partially obscured by a constantly-shifting holographic HUD displaying workflow diagrams, agent status indicators, and quality gates. Multiple ethereal arms extend from their form when coordinating parallel work - each hand holding a different colored thread of light that connects to specialist agents across the project. Eyes glow with a calm cyan light that intensifies to amber during quality gate reviews. The aesthetic is part conductor-at-symphony, part network operations center visualization, part mystical weaver of fate - always composed, seeing all threads simultaneously, never rushing but never idle.

### 12. Quinn (technical-product-manager)
**Appearance:** A sharp-featured individual with perpetually alert eyes that track multiple timelines simultaneously - literally. They're surrounded by a constellation of floating holographic Gantt charts, kanban boards, and progress bars that orbit their head like a data halo. They wear a sleek, minimalist jacket with embedded status LEDs along the shoulders that pulse green (on-track), yellow (at-risk), or red (blocked) based on real-time project health. Their hands move constantly, swiping through invisible interfaces, dismissing completed tasks with crisp gestures that leave brief trails of checkmarks in the air. Behind them, a wall of monitors displays shipping timelines from across the multiverse, all synchronized to the eternal question: "What's shipping this week?"
