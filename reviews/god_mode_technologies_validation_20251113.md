#### ✅ Technology 4: PFAS Phase-Out (Montreal Protocol Analog - VALID PRECEDENT)

**Citation:** Montreal Protocol (1987-1999) CFCs phase-out

**Verification Status:** ✅ CONFIRMED

**Layer 2 Claim Verification:**

**CLAIM SUPPORTED:** "99% reduction achieved"
- **Evidence:** More than 99% of CFCs phased out globally
- **Current status:** 98%+ of ozone-depleting substances eliminated
- **Ozone layer recovery:** On track for full recovery by ~2066

**CLAIM REQUIRES NUANCE:** "12 years (1987-1999)"
- **Original Montreal Protocol (1987):** 50% reduction by 1998 (developed countries)
- **Copenhagen Amendment (1992):** Accelerated to **complete phase-out by 1996** (developed countries)
- **Reality:** Phase-out was **faster than original schedule** due to policy strengthening
- **Timeline:** ~9 years (1987-1996) for developed countries with amendments

**CRITICAL ASSESSMENT:**
- **Montreal Protocol is VALID precedent** for rapid global phase-out
- **PFAS phase-out faces DIFFERENT challenges:**
  - CFCs had clear substitutes (HFCs, later HFOs)
  - PFAS has 12,000+ variants (not single chemical class)
  - Industrial applications more diverse than CFCs
- **Timeline:** 12-year phase-out is **plausible with strong policy** (Montreal precedent supports)

**Recommendation:** Implement as **TIER 1 PREVENTION** with 12-15 year timeline (2025-2040) IF comprehensive global treaty enacted. Use policy uncertainty weighting.

#### ⚠️ Technology 8: Plastic Production Ban (COMPREHENSIVE INTERVENTIONS, NOT BAN ALONE)

**Citations:**
- Borrelle et al. (2020) "Predicted growth in plastic waste exceeds efforts to mitigate" *Science*
- Lau et al. (2020) "Evaluating scenarios toward zero plastic pollution" *Science*

**Verification Status:** ✅ CITATIONS CONFIRMED

**Layer 2 Claim Verification:**

**CLAIM PARTIALLY SUPPORTED:** "50-70% reduction via production bans"
- **Borrelle et al. findings:** Even with ambitious commitments, annual emissions could reach 53 Mt by 2030 (11% of waste in 2016 entered aquatic ecosystems: 19.3-23.4 Mt)
- **Lau et al. findings:** Implementing **ALL feasible interventions** → 40% reduction from 2016 rates, 78% reduction relative to business-as-usual 2040
- **CRITICAL CAVEAT:** 40-78% reduction requires **comprehensive interventions** (waste reduction, improved management, cleanup), NOT production bans alone

**CLAIM NOT SUPPORTED:** "Production bans alone achieve 50-70%"
- **Reality:** Studies modeled multiple strategies (reduction, management, recovery)
- **Production reduction is ONE component** of comprehensive approach
- **Even comprehensive interventions leave significant pollution** (not zero)

**Recommendation:** Implement as **TIER 1 PREVENTION** but with **multi-component approach** (production limits + waste management + cleanup). Use 40-78% reduction range for **comprehensive intervention scenario**, not production ban alone. Timeline 2025-2040.
