Remove the far-future speculation (BCIs/merger) but preserve the critical middle ground of wearable/AR/VR augmentation that will define the next decade. ## Technology Timeline Analysis

### Near-term (10-36 months / 2025-2028)

**What's Deployable:**
- **Apple Vision Pro Gen 3-4** reaching $1000 price point with all-day battery
- **Meta's EMG wristbands** (CTRL-labs acquisition) enabling thought-to-text at 50+ WPM
- **AI tutors with persistent memory** - Claude/GPT agents that remember years of interaction
- **Pharmaceutical cognitive enhancement** - Modafinil variants, targeted nootropic stacks (already happening in Silicon Valley)
- **Haptic feedback suits** for embodied AI collaboration (Teslasuit, HaptX already commercial)

Current research by Meta Reality Labs suggests non-invasive neural interfaces via EMG can achieve 90% accuracy on 1000+ word vocabularies. Stanford's recent work on AI-assisted problem solving shows 40% improvement in complex reasoning tasks. These aren't speculative - they're in labs NOW.

**Pathway from Current to Deeper Integration:**
The key insight from William Gibson: "The future is already here, it's just not evenly distributed." We're already seeing:
- Programmers using Copilot/Cursor achieving 3-5x productivity
- Knowledge workers with GPT-4 custom agents outperforming those without
- VR training producing 4x faster skill acquisition (PwC study, 2024)

The augmentation divide is ALREADY forming. It's not about invasive BCIs - it's about who has access to the $3000/month AI toolkit.

### Mid-term (36-72 months / 2028-2031)

**BCI Commercial Viability:**
Neuralink and Synchron are at TRL 4-5 NOW (2025). Following typical medical device timelines:
- FDA approval for paralysis/disability: 2027-2028 (already in trials)
- Off-label use by wealthy early adopters: 2029-2030
- BUT: Only ~10,000 people globally by 2031 (compare LASIK adoption curve)

The real story isn't BCIs - it's **non-invasive alternatives** racing ahead:
- Ultrasound neuromodulation (focused ultrasound through skull)
- Temporal interference stimulation (non-invasive deep brain stimulation)
- Optogenetics via engineered viral vectors (no surgery required)

**Genetic Enhancement Timeline:**
He Jiankui's CRISPR babies (2018) proved feasibility but created regulatory backlash. Current trajectory:
- Germline editing banned in most nations through 2030+
- Somatic gene therapy for adults accelerating (CRISPR 3.0 with base/prime editing)
- "Cognitive enhancement" hidden as "Alzheimer's prevention" (APOE4 editing)

Neal Stephenson's *Seveneves* depicts this perfectly: enhancement happens first as medical treatment, then spreads.

### Long-term (72-120 months / 2031-2035)

**External → Internal Inflection Point:**
Drawing from Ramez Naam's *Nexus* trilogy and recent DARPA N3 program results:

The transition happens via **reversible augmentation** first:
1. Injectable neural mesh (Lieber/Harvard, dissolves if needed)
2. Neural dust (Berkeley, ultrasound-powered microchips)
3. Endovascular stents (Synchron, no open-brain surgery)

By 2035, we're not talking "merger" but **"neural API access"** - the ability to query AI systems with thought at broadband speeds. This creates three classes:
- **Purely augmented** (1%): Direct neural interface
- **Externally augmented** (30%): AR/VR/wearables
- **Unaugmented** (69%): Economic/cultural barriers

This mirrors smartphone adoption (2007-2017) but with higher stakes.

## Hard vs Soft Science Fiction Boundary

### Hard Sci-Fi (Model These - TRL 3-7)

**The Gibson Zone** (incremental but transformative):
- **AR contact lenses** (Mojo Vision prototypes exist)
- **Subvocal interfaces** (MIT AlterEgo achieving 92% accuracy)
- **Transcranial stimulation** (tDCS/TMS already commercial)
- **AI-mediated memory palaces** (Method of loci + AI generation)
- **Swarm intelligence tools** (human-AI team coordination)

These create the *Rainbow's End* scenario (Vernor Vinge): augmented humans so capable they're effectively a different species, but through tools rather than biology.

**Critical insight:** The augmentation that matters isn't processing speed but **access to AI capabilities**. A human with GPT-7 whispering in their ear beats a human with 2x biological IQ.

### Soft Sci-Fi (Don't Model - TRL 0-2)

**The Egan Zone** (requires new physics/biology):
- Consciousness uploading (no theory of consciousness)
- Hive minds (no bandwidth for genuine merger)
- Post-biological intelligence (undefined end state)
- Quantum consciousness (Penrose-Hameroff unproven)

Greg Egan's *Diaspora* is brilliant speculation but there's no research pathway from here to there. These belong in far-future fiction, not 10-year models.

## Scenario Utility Evaluation

### High-Value Scenarios for Modeling

**A. Enhancement Stratification (CRITICAL TO MODEL)**
- Already happening with AI tools ($20k/year for full stack)
- AR glasses will cost $3000+ initially (Apple Vision trajectory)
- Creates persistent advantage (enhanced kids → enhanced adults)
- Research basis: Digital divide studies, OECD inequality reports

This is THE scenario for 2025-2035. It's not speculative - it's extrapolation.

**B. Enhancement Arms Race**
Drawing from *The Expanse* (Martian military enhancement):
- China already investing $15B in brain-computer interface research
- US DARPA N3 program developing non-invasive neural interfaces
- Corporate enhancement for competitive advantage (Wall Street, Silicon Valley)

Military and economic competition WILL drive adoption despite risks.

**C. Regulatory Patchwork**
EU banning enhancement while Singapore embraces it. Creates:
- Enhancement havens (medical tourism for cognition)
- Underground markets (dark web nootropics already $2B market)
- Geopolitical shifts (enhanced vs unenhanced nations)

### Low-Value Scenarios (Too Speculative)

- Universal enhancement (no historical precedent for equal tech distribution)
- Complete enhancement ban (impossible to enforce, see drug war)
- Sudden merger/uploading (no technological pathway)

1. **Missing the inequality tipping point** - When 10% are augmented, society bifurcates
2. **Ignoring military/corporate drivers** - These push adoption despite regulation

This is sufficient to model the critical dynamics without venturing into uploading fantasies.
