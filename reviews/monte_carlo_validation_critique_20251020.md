1. **Crisis correlation:** Assumes all crises happen simultaneously (water + food + energy + social)

2. **Response paralysis:** Assumes no adaptive response during crisis
   - Reality: Crisis triggers innovation (WWII → computers, COVID → mRNA vaccines)

3. **Homogeneous failure:** All 20 AI agents fail similarly
   - Reality: Heterogeneous systems have uncorrelated failure modes

**Kahneman & Tversky (1979) - Prospect Theory:** Humans become risk-seeking under losses. The simulation assumes passive acceptance of decline.
