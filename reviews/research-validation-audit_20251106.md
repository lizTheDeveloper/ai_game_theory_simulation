## Key Findings: Research Conflicts

### Conflict 1: Adaptation Optimism vs Pessimism

**Optimistic evidence (2024):**
- Ballester et al. (2024): 80% mortality reduction from adaptation in Europe
- Vicedo-Cabrera et al. (2022): Projections overestimate by 20-80% without adaptation

**Pessimistic evidence (ongoing):**
- Wet-bulb temperature limits remain absolute (>35°C = unsurvivable)
- Infrastructure adaptation requires wealth + time (not universal)
- Behavioral adaptation limited at extreme temperatures

**Resolution:** Both are true. Adaptation works within limits but has hard constraints. Simulation should model:
- Strong adaptation effects at moderate heat stress (<32°C wet-bulb)
- Diminishing returns approaching physiological limits (>35°C)
- Infrastructure-dependent effectiveness (wealth, governance thresholds)

### Conflict 2: AI Alignment Difficulty

**Optimistic signals:**
- Constitutional AI, RLHF showing practical progress
- Weak-to-strong generalization research (Burns et al. 2023)
- Mechanistic interpretability advances

**Pessimistic signals:**
- Alignment faking in Claude (Greenblatt et al. 2024)
- Sandbagging capabilities demonstrated
- Open problems in scalable oversight remain unsolved

**Resolution:** Both are true. Alignment has partial solutions for current systems but fundamental challenges remain for superhuman AI. Simulation correctly models this tension via adversarial evaluation system.

### Conflict 3: Cooperative Economics Effectiveness

**Evidence for effectiveness:**
- Québec data: 1.77× survival advantage (62% vs 35% at 5 years)
- Borzaga (2014): Crisis resilience in Italian cooperatives

**Evidence of limitations:**
- Small sample sizes (N=21 platform coops)
- Non-AI sectors (extrapolation required)
- Governance overhead (Mannan & Pek 2024)

**Resolution:** Effect exists but magnitude uncertain. Simulation correctly uses wide uncertainty bounds (±40-50%) and conservative parameter choices (1.5× instead of 1.77×).

**No resolution required** - conflicts acknowledged and handled appropriately.
