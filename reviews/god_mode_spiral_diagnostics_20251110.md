## Critical Insight: The Governance Gap

**Observation:** Technologies that directly affect material/energy/health are working (>100%), but social systems are failing:
- Physical safety: 0% (conflict, violence)
- Information integrity: 0% (misinformation, propaganda)
- Democratic spiral: INACTIVE (governance not improving)

**Hypothesis Validated:** Sylvia's research (research/GOD_MODE_ANALYSIS_model_mechanisms_20251110.md) predicted:
> "Hidden Dependencies: Tech might require conditions the collapsed world can't provide:
> - Stable governments (6.6% political freedom)
> - Economic capacity (1% material abundance)
> - Social coordination (trust dynamics collapsed)"

**Result:** Political freedom is 72.8% (not collapsed), but physical safety and information integrity are 0%. This suggests:
1. **Technology deployment ≠ technology effectiveness** (tech exists but isn't being applied to safety/information)
2. **Social systems can't absorb rapid change** (12 months is too fast for institutional adaptation)
3. **Conflict/misinformation overwhelm tech benefits** (negative spirals dominate early)

## Recommendations for Phase 2 (Scenario Definition System)

### Scenario Types to Test

1. **Early Start Scenarios** (test time constant hypothesis)
   - Deploy tech at month -24 (2 years before 2025 start)
   - Deploy tech at month -120 (10 years early)
   - Question: Does starting earlier allow spirals to establish?

2. **Governance-First Scenarios** (test dependency hypothesis)
   - Boost governance quality to 0.8 before tech deployment
   - Ensure physical safety >0.5 and information integrity >0.5
   - Question: Do social foundations enable tech effectiveness?

3. **Sequenced Deployment Scenarios** (test absorption capacity)
   - Deploy tech in waves (TIER 0 → TIER 1 → TIER 2 → ...)
   - 12-24 month gaps between tiers
   - Question: Does gradual deployment allow institutions to adapt?

4. **Spiral-Targeted Scenarios** (test activation thresholds)
   - Manually boost specific QoL dimensions to threshold levels
   - Force 3+ spirals active simultaneously
   - Question: Are spiral activation conditions too stringent?
