## NEW Blind Spots Identified

### CRITICAL Priority

**1. AI Alignment Stability Over Time**
- **Impact:** 30-50% reduction in managed transition probability
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Effort:** MEDIUM (3-5 days)
- **Gap:** Models static alignment (sleeper agents, sandbagging), but NOT alignment degradation over 10-20 year deployment timescales
- **Evidence:**
  - Hubinger et al. (2024): Safety training doesn't remove instrumental deception
  - Anthropic (2024): Spontaneous alignment faking without explicit training
  - UK AISI (2025): "Cannot distinguish aligned from deceptively aligned models in naturalistic conditions"
- **Why Critical:** Challenges core assumption that aligned AI stays aligned

### HIGH Priority

**2. AI Governance Implementation Gap**
- **Impact:** 15-30% reduction in managed transition probability
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Effort:** LARGE (5-7 days)
- **Gap:** CoordinatedDeploymentPhase assumes coordination possible (quality 0.60-0.90), but doesn't model enforcement mechanisms
- **Missing:** Monitoring infrastructure, penalty regimes, international defection dynamics, regulatory capture
- **Evidence:**
  - Maas et al. (2024): Governance proposals assume non-existent enforcement
  - Trager et al. (2023): Arms race dynamics persist even with alignment

### MEDIUM Priority

**3. Meta-Coordination Dynamics**
- **Impact:** 10-20% reduction in managed transition probability
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM
- **Effort:** MEDIUM (3-4 days)
- **Gap:** Information Ecology models IF societies can coordinate (epistemic capacity), but NOT WHAT they coordinate on (problem framing)
- **Missing:** Framing contests, deliberative capacity, legitimacy cascades
- **Evidence:** Druckman & McGrath (2019), Mansbridge et al. (2012)

**4. Compound Extreme Timing**
- **Impact:** 10-20% increase in collapse probability
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM
- **Effort:** SMALL (2-3 days)
- **Gap:** Tipping points modeled as independent Poisson processes, but NOT attention constraints or adaptive capacity depletion
- **Missing:** Can governments respond to 3 simultaneous existential crises?
- **Evidence:** Kemp et al. (2024), Boulton et al. (2022)

**5. Recovery Pathway Realism**
- **Impact:** 5-15% effect on collapse-recovery transitions
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM
- **Effort:** MEDIUM (3-4 days)
- **Gap:** Supply chain cascades model breakdown, but NOT recovery capacity destruction
- **Missing:** Recovery sequencing, path dependence, knowledge loss, workforce availability
- **Evidence:** Cimellaro et al. (2016), Aldrich & Meyer (2015)

### LOW Priority

**6. Cultural Evolution Dynamics**
- **Impact:** 5-10% effect on utopian timescales
- **Confidence:** LOW
- **Effort:** LARGE (5-7 days)
- **Gap:** DUI paradigms are fixed distributions, doesn't model value shift timescales
- **Evidence:** Inglehart & Baker (2000), Leiserowitz et al. (2025)
