## Completed Evaluations

### 1. Dystopia-Utopia Index (DUI)
**Verdict:** FAIL - Do not proceed
**Document:** `/reviews/dystopia_utopia_index_critique_20251019.md`

**Critical Flaws:**
- **Aggregation Fallacy**: Cannot mathematically combine freedom vs food security trade-offs (Ravallion 2012)
- **Data Fabrication**: Authoritarian regimes overstate by 35% (Martinez 2022)
- **Western Bias**: Singapore "dystopian" despite 85% satisfaction
- **Missing Dystopia**: No measurement of surveillance capitalism, meaninglessness epidemic (Graeber 2018)
- **Paradox**: Places dystopian enough to measure lack measurement infrastructure

**Alternative Approaches Proposed:**
1. Minimal suffering indicators (death, displacement)
2. Multiple paradigms (Western, Development, Ecological, Indigenous)
3. Abandon country-level quantification

### 2. Prevention Mechanisms
**Verdict:** CONDITIONAL PASS - Reduce parameters by 2-3x
**Document:** `/reviews/prevention_mechanisms_critique_20251019.md`

**Agreements:**
- Prevention > Recovery is correct (95% confidence)
- Focus on cascades is appropriate
- 2% humane utopia window is realistic

**Critical Adjustments Needed:**
- **Cascade Oversimplification**: Only 18% achieve cascades (Sovacool 2024), not automatic at 5-20%
- **False Positives**: Early warning has 40-60% false positive rate (Boers & Rypdal 2021)
- **Missing Negative Cascades**: Green backlash (Yellow Vests, Dutch farmers)
- **Resource Bottlenecks**: Lithium 42x demand vs 1.5x production growth (IEA 2024)

**Recommendation:** Implement with cascade probability 10% at 5% penetration (not automatic), add political backlash dynamics.

### 3. Technology Diffusion (Fix #9)
**Verdict:** CONDITIONAL PASS - Cap acceleration at 30%
**Document:** `/reviews/technology_diffusion_critique_20251019.md`

**Research Quality:** EXCELLENT - Bass model, TAM meta-analyses, recent AI studies all properly cited

**Critical Contradiction:**
- States: "70% of challenges are people/process" (correct)
- Claims: "AI accelerates by 40-56%" (impossible)
- Math: If 70% is organizational, max acceleration is 30%, not 56%

**Missing Elements:**
- Implementation dip (Brynjolfsson's J-Curve)
- Regional trust variation (China 78%, USA 35%)
- Network effects and lock-in
- Technical debt accumulation

**Recommendation:** Implement with bounded acceleration model, separate technical (30%) from organizational (70%) components.

## Methodological Patterns Identified

### Recurring Issues in Research

1. **Selection Bias**
   - Studies examine only successful cases
   - Survivorship bias in innovation diffusion
   - Cherry-picking supportive evidence

2. **Western-Centric Assumptions**
   - Individual freedom > collective harmony
   - GDP/wealth as proxy for development
   - Democracy as only legitimate governance

3. **Linear Thinking in Complex Systems**
   - Assuming monotonic improvement
   - Ignoring negative feedback loops
   - Missing cascade competition

4. **Over-Optimism About Technology**
   - Assuming capability = adoption
   - Ignoring organizational inertia
   - Underestimating political resistance

5. **False Precision**
   - Exact parameters from uncertain data
   - No uncertainty bands
   - Treating estimates as facts

## Recommendations for Project

### Methodological Improvements

1. **Add Uncertainty Bands**
   - All parameters should have ±25-30% ranges
   - Sensitivity analysis for key assumptions
   - Multiple scenario modeling

2. **Model Competition Not Just Cooperation**
   - Positive vs negative cascade competition
   - Political backlash dynamics
   - Resource constraint conflicts

3. **Separate Technical from Human Factors**
   - Technical work: AI-accelerable
   - Organizational work: Resistant to automation
   - Political work: May oppose automation

4. **Regional and Cultural Variation**
   - Not all societies value same outcomes
   - Trust varies dramatically by culture
   - Implementation capacity differs

5. **Accept Measurement Limitations**
   - Some concepts (dystopia) resist quantification
   - False precision worse than honest uncertainty
   - Focus on measurable suffering over abstract indices
