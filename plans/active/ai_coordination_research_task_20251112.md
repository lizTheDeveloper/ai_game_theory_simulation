## Research Questions (Priority Order)

### 1. Historical Transition Mortality (HIGHEST PRIORITY)

**Question:** What are mortality bounds for rapid economic/technological transitions (coerced vs. supported)?

**Current Hypothesis:**
- God mode (instant, uncoordinated): 30% mortality (2.44B deaths)
- AI-coordinated (phased, supported): <5% mortality target
- Baseline: Great Leap Forward 15-55M deaths (1-3% of China's population)

**What You Need:**
- Historical case studies with mortality data:
  - Great Leap Forward (coerced agricultural transition)
  - Green Revolution (agricultural technology diffusion)
  - Industrial Revolution transitions
  - Post-Soviet economic collapse (1990s Russia)
  - Rapid urbanization events (China 1980-2020)
- Mortality rates by transition type: coerced vs. voluntary vs. supported
- Timescale vs. mortality relationship (instant vs. decades)
- What made some transitions deadly vs. benign?

**Key Question:** Is 30% mortality for instant uncoordinated deployment reasonable? What's plausible for coordinated?

**Search Terms:** "Great Leap Forward mortality", "rapid economic transition deaths", "technology diffusion mortality", "post-Soviet collapse mortality", "agricultural transition famine"

---

### 2. Technology Diffusion Rates (CRITICAL)

**Question:** How fast can transformative technologies actually deploy without causing crisis?

**What You Need:**
- Empirical technology diffusion curves (S-curves, not hype cycles)
- Economic adaptation timescales (job retraining, capital reallocation)
- Infrastructure deployment rates (energy grids, transportation)
- Historical analogues: Electrification, internet, mobile phones, renewables
- What factors determine safe diffusion speed? (training, capital, social acceptance)

**Key Parameters:**
- Typical diffusion time: 0-100% adoption
- "Safe" diffusion rate (avoids crisis)
- Critical thresholds (when does speed cause mortality?)

**Search Terms:** "technology diffusion rates historical", "infrastructure deployment timeline", "economic adaptation speed", "disruptive technology adoption curve", "safe technology transition speed"

---

### 3. Social Safety Net Effectiveness (HIGH PRIORITY)

**Question:** Can social support reduce transition mortality? By how much?

**What You Need:**
- Empirical studies on safety net effectiveness during economic disruption
- Universal Basic Income pilots during automation/crisis (Kenya, Finland, etc.)
- Job retraining program success rates
- Healthcare access impact on transition mortality
- Quantitative effectiveness: Safety net reduces mortality by X%?

**Search Terms:** "social safety net economic transition", "UBI pilot results", "job retraining effectiveness", "healthcare access mortality reduction", "unemployment insurance crisis"

---

### 4. Regional Readiness Assessment (IMPORTANT)

**Question:** What determines if a region can handle rapid technological change without collapse?

**What You Need:**
- Resilience frameworks for technological transition
- Vulnerability indicators (governance capacity, infrastructure, education)
- Historical patterns: Why did some regions adapt while others collapsed?
- Regional capacity scoring systems (if any exist)

**Search Terms:** "regional resilience technology transition", "governance capacity rapid change", "infrastructure readiness technological disruption", "vulnerability indicators economic shock"

---

### 5. AI Coordination Mechanisms (ASPIRATIONAL)

**Question:** How could aligned AI coordinate technology deployment? (Less critical - may not have research yet)

**What You Need:**
- AI-assisted logistics/supply chain optimization case studies
- Multi-agent coordination frameworks
- Decision support systems for complex transitions
- Any speculative but grounded proposals for AI-managed transitions

**Search Terms:** "AI coordination logistics", "multi-agent deployment optimization", "AI-assisted supply chain crisis", "algorithmic resource allocation"
