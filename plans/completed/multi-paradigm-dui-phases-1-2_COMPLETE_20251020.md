## Implementation Strategy (Oct 20, 2025)

**Status:** COMPLETE (design phase)

### Three-Tier Architecture

**Tier 1: Simulation Foundation (What Drives Outcomes)**
- Environmental boundaries, social cohesion, technology, economy, geopolitics
- **Already implemented** in existing simulation (`src/simulation/`)

**Tier 2A: High-Confidence Paradigms (Drive + Report)**
- **Western Liberal:** V-Dem democracy, Freedom House (202 countries, HIGH confidence)
- **Development Needs:** HDI, MPI, healthcare, food (193 countries, HIGH confidence)
- **Ecological Harmony:** Planetary boundaries, footprint, air quality (188 countries, MEDIUM-HIGH confidence)

**Tier 2B: Reporting-Only Paradigms (Diagnostic Lens)**
- **Indigenous/Communitarian:** Derives from social cohesion mechanics + WVS proxies
  - Does NOT drive simulation (uses existing mechanics)
  - Reports what conventional metrics miss (community solidarity, cultural continuity)
  - Makes visible the measurement gap (199/200 countries lack GNH-equivalent)

### Key Design Decisions

1. **Separation of Concerns:**
   - Simulation mechanics (what happens) ≠ Diagnostic reporting (how we interpret it)
   - Indigenous paradigm doesn't add new mechanics, reinterprets existing social cohesion

2. **Zero-Handling Solution:**
   - `MIN_FLOOR = 0.1` prevents geometric mean breakdown
   - North Korea V-Dem 0.02 → 0.1 (preserves non-compensatory property)
   - Documented as methodological choice in research

3. **Air Quality Addition:**
   - 13th Ecological indicator (PM2.5, WHO 2024)
   - 7M deaths/year globally - can't omit from ecological paradigm
   - Thresholds: Utopia <5 μg/m³, Dystopia >50 μg/m³

4. **Research Advocacy Strategy:**
   - Make measurement gap visible: "Only Bhutan measures communitarian wellbeing"
   - Policy argument: "If we only measure GDP/HDI, we optimize for GDP/HDI"
   - Enables 3 research papers on measurement gaps

### Government System Integration (CRITICAL)

**30 real-world governments already implemented (Oct 2025):**
- State capacity data (WGI 2024: effectiveness, corruption, regulatory quality)
- Government types (parliamentary, presidential, authoritarian, etc.)
