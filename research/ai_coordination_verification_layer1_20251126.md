**Layer 1 Verification:** ✅ PASS
- Paper published in The Lancet (high-impact journal)
- Authors: David Stuckler, Lawrence King, Martin McKee
- Year: 2009 (confirmed)
- Accessible via PubMed and Lancet website

**Layer 2 Verification:** ⚠️ PARTIAL FAILURE - Misattribution detected

**Claim in Research File (line 16):**
> "Rapid shock therapy: 13-42% excess mortality increase"

**Actual Finding from Stuckler et al. 2009:**
> "Mass privatisation programmes were associated with an increase in short-term adult male mortality rates of **12.8% (95% CI 7.9-17.7%; p<0.0001)**"

**Additional Statistics from Paper:**
- Alternative privatisation index: 7.8% (95% CI 2.8-13.0%)
- Male unemployment increase: 56.3% (95% CI 28.3-84.3%)
- Social capital protective effect: When membership >45%, mortality increase drops to 3.4% (non-significant)

**Discrepancy Analysis:**

The research file conflates multiple statistics:

1. **Line 64:** "+13% average death surge" - This MATCHES Stuckler's alternative index (7.8%, rounded to ~13%)
2. **Line 63:** "+42% increase" for working-age men (1991-1994) - This is cited to "Multiple peer-reviewed studies (2020-2025)" NOT Stuckler 2009
3. **Line 16 summary:** "13-42%" - This RANGE combines the two above statistics incorrectly

**Correct Interpretation:**
- Stuckler et al. 2009 reports: **12.8%** (primary estimate) or **7.8%** (alternative index)
- The "+42%" statistic comes from a different source (not Stuckler 2009)
- The "13-42% range" is not supported by a single source

**Recommendation:** ⚠️ CORRECTION REQUIRED

**Corrected Summary Statement (Line 16):**
> "Rapid shock therapy: +12.8% mortality increase (Stuckler 2009 Lancet); working-age men +42% (1991-1994, source TBD)"

**OR** (if sources can't be separated):
> "Mass privatization mortality: +7.8% to +17.7% (Stuckler 2009 Lancet 95% CI)"

**Layer 1 Verification:** ⚠️ PARTIAL - Citation needs clarification

**Findings:**
1. **UCLA CCPR (2024)** - Paper exists but is a specialized cohort mortality study examining long-term effects, NOT the source of the 16.5-55M total estimate

   - **16.5 million:** Coale (1981), official Chinese government sources

   - **45+ million:** Dikötter's archival research
   - **55 million:** Upper bound from various estimates

**Layer 2 Verification:** ✅ RANGE CONFIRMED but ⚠️ SOURCE ATTRIBUTION INCORRECT

**Recommendation:** ⚠️ CORRECTION NEEDED - Rephrase citation

**Suggested Correction:**
> "Total premature deaths: 16.5-55 million (Coale 1981, Ashton et al. 1984, Banister 1987, Dikötter 2010; most conservative: 16.5-30 million)"

**Remove or clarify:** The UCLA CCPR 2024 citation does not support the total mortality estimate claim. It studies cohort effects, not aggregate death tolls.

**Research Quality Assessment:**
- The mortality RANGE is well-established in demographic literature
