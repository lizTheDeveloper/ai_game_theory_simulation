## Conclusion

The author clearly engaged with peer-reviewed sources, accurately reported major findings, and correctly identified key risks. However, the simulation parameters (Section 5) contain EXTENSIVE unsourced quantitative estimates that give false impression of empirical precision.

**Primary Recommendation:** Document should be REVISED to clearly distinguish:
1. Empirical findings from sources (keep as-is)
2. Author extrapolations (add disclaimers)
3. Unknown quantities (state "unknown - classified" instead of inventing numbers)

**Secondary Recommendation:** For simulation purposes, treat ALL probability estimates as PLACEHOLDERS requiring calibration, not as empirical constants. The qualitative causal chains (weak-to-moderate, indirect, multiple circuit breakers) are well-supported. The specific percentages are educated guesses.

**Fitness for Purpose:** Despite issues, this document IS suitable for parameterizing a Phase 1A simulation IF probability estimates are treated as initial guesses subject to sensitivity analysis, not as validated empirical constants.
