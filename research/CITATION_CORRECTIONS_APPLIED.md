## Citation Format Standards Applied

All corrected citations now follow these formats:

### In-text citations:
- **Journal/Conference:** "Author et al. (Year), Journal: 'Title'"
- **arXiv papers:** "Author et al. (Year), arXiv:YYMM.NNNNN: 'Title'"
- **Blog posts:** "Author (Year): 'Title' (Blog Post)"
- **Forum posts:** "Platform (Year): 'Title'"

### References section:
- **arXiv:** "N. **Author et al. (Year)**, arXiv:YYMM.NNNNN: 'Title' [newline] - https://arxiv.org/abs/YYMM.NNNNN"
- **Blog/Forum:** "N. **Platform/Author (Year)**: 'Title' (Type) [newline] - [URL]"
