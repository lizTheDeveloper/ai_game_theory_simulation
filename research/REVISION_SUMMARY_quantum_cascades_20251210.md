### CRITICAL-2: 20x Speedup Claim Overstatement

**Problem:** IonQ/AstraZeneca 20x speedup cited as evidence of quantum-AI convergence without qualification that this was a narrow, carefully selected task.

**Changes Made:**

1. **Capability multiplier reductions:**
   - Research: 5-20x → **2-10x** (MEDIUM-HIGH → LOW-MEDIUM confidence)
   - Physical: 10-100x → **5-50x** (HIGH → LOW-MEDIUM confidence)
   - Cognitive: 1-3x → **1-2x** (LOW-MEDIUM → VERY LOW confidence)

2. **Added skeptical academic voices:**
   - **Sankar Das Sarma (Maryland):** Drug discovery claims "baffling" given quantum chemistry is "minuscule part of the whole process"; claims of speedups in finance/ML/drug discovery have "highly unsatisfying evidence"
   - **Scott Aaronson (UT Austin):** "Skepticism was always warranted regarding claims about how quantum computing will revolutionize machine learning, optimization, and finance"
   - **Classical competition:** Flatiron Institute (2024) simulated IBM's 127-qubit Eagle processor with greater accuracy than quantum device, on a laptop

3. **Added critical caveats:**
   - 20x speedup was for **Suzuki-Miyaura cross-coupling reaction simulation** - highly specific task
   - Narrow applicability: Multipliers apply only to quantum-amenable problems, NOT general AI
   - Error correction overhead reduces effective speedups

4. **Timeline extensions:**
   - Research capability enhancement: 2028-2035 → **2035-2045**
   - Physical capability enhancement: 2028-2035 → **2035-2045**

**Sources added:**
- MIT Technology Review (Das Sarma critique)
- Scott Aaronson Blog (ML/optimization skepticism)
- QuEra (Q2B24 conference, Aaronson/Preskill skeptical on AI speedups)
- IEEE Spectrum (classical computing catching up)

### SIGNIFICANT-1: Social Trust Recovery Weak Evidence

**Problem:** Trust recovery timeline extrapolated from non-analogous events (Equifax breach, Y2K) without acknowledging fundamental differences.

**Changes Made:**

1. **Confidence downgrade:**
   - Trust recovery timeline: LOW → **VERY LOW**

2. **Timeline extension:**
   - Trust recovery: 5-15 years → **10-30 years**
   - Added caveat: "may never fully recover to pre-crisis baseline"

3. **Critical analog weakness analysis added:**
   - **Equifax weakness:** 143M records ≠ global cryptographic infrastructure collapse; single company failure ≠ systemic architectural failure
   - **Y2K weakness:** Proactive 5-year preparation with known deadline ≠ reactive chaotic response with zero warning
   - **Missing negative cases:** No examination of permanent trust deficits, technology avoidance, multi-generational skepticism

4. **Recovery timescale table revision:**
   - Added "Confidence" column
   - Critical infrastructure patching: 6-12 months → **6-18 months** (HIGH → LOW confidence)
   - Majority PQC adoption: 5-10 years → **10-20 years** (MEDIUM confidence)
   - Full infrastructure replacement: 10-20 years → **20-40 years** (MEDIUM → LOW confidence)
   - Social trust recovery: 5-15 years → **10-30 years** (LOW → VERY LOW confidence)

**Impact:** Acknowledges that trust recovery may take decades or may be permanently impaired, representing ongoing economic drag.
