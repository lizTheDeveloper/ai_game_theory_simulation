### Section 1: One-Shot Attempts (Obvious Threat)

#### Claim 1.1: "Untrained employees: 20% click malicious links (baseline)"
- **Status**: ⚠️ **Partially Verified**
- **Sources Checked**: Hoxhunt reports, Sprinto statistics compilations
- **Verification Result**:
  - Web search found: "Without security awareness training, 34.3% of employees are likely to click on malicious links" (Sprinto blog citing industry data)
  - Hospital study: "95 simulated phishing campaigns comprising 2,971,945 emails produced an overall median click rate of 16.7% across 6 hospitals"
  - North America baseline: "Users in North America posted a 25.5% click rate"
  - **Context Issue**: 20% is within documented range (16.7%-34.3%), but **no specific source citation** provided in original document
- **Grade**: **Moderate - plausible baseline, lacks specific source**

#### Claim 1.2: "After security training: 3.2% failure rate after 1 year (86% reduction)"
- **Status**: ⚠️ **Partially Verified**
- **Sources Checked**: Hoxhunt 2024 Phishing Trends Report
- **Verification Result**:
  - Web search found: "Microsoft Digital Defense Report observed that awareness training by itself yielded only about a 3% reduction in phishing click rates on average"
  - Hoxhunt 2024 data: AI-generated phishing: 2.9% (March 2023) → 2.1% (Nov 2024) → 2.78% (March 2025)
  - Hoxhunt data: Human-written phishing: 4.2% → 2.3% → 2.25% over same period
  - Industry benchmark: "Organizations with highly mature phishing programs can get their click rate to around 1-2%"
  - **Context Issue**: 3.2% aligns with industry data, but **"86% reduction" claim not directly verified** (would require 3.2% from 22.9% baseline, which is plausible but not explicitly stated)
- **Grade**: **Moderate - figure aligns with industry benchmarks, but 86% reduction not independently verified**

#### Claim 1.3: "Stubbornly resistant group: 1.5% median click rate even after repeated training"
- **Status**: ⚠️ **Partially Verified**
- **Sources Checked**: Sprinto statistics, Hoxhunt reports
- **Verification Result**:
  - **DIRECT QUOTE FOUND**: Sprinto blog states: "In phishing simulations, a stubborn median of 1.5% of employees still click dangerous links even after repeated awareness training"
  - Industry guidance confirms: "Organizations with highly mature phishing programs can get their click rate to around 1-2%"

  - **Interpretation Issue**: Original document implies "stubbornly resistant," but industry data suggests this represents **new hires, not resistant veterans**
- **Grade**: **Good - statistic verified, but interpretation slightly misleading**

#### Claim 1.4: "IT staff (supposedly trained): Still receive 40 targeted attacks/year"
- **Status**: ❓ **Cannot Verify Specific Number**

- **Verification Result**:
  - Related finding: "IT leaders identify finance professionals (27%) and IT team members (23%) as the individuals within their organizations most likely to be targeted"
