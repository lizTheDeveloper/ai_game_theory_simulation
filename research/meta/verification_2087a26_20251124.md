## Summary

BaselineMortalityPhase adds baseline demographic mortality (natural deaths from aging/disease) to the Bayesian mortality system using UN World Population Prospects 2024 historical crude death rate (CDR) data.

**Purpose:** Fix hindcast Phase 3 blocker where population was declining (5.3B→2.7B) instead of growing (5.3B→6.1B) during 1990-2000.

## Architecture Context

**Phase order:** 34.8 (before BayesianMortalityResolutionPhase at 35.0)

**Key architectural decision:** ERA multipliers are NOT applied to baseline mortality.

**Rationale from code:**
```typescript
// ERA multipliers represent "crisis response capability" not "baseline health"
// 1990 had higher baseline mortality but LOWER crisis response capability
```

**Research question for validation:**
- Is the separation of "baseline health" (improving over time) and "crisis response capability" (also improving) conceptually sound?
- Should historical healthcare improvements be modeled differently than crisis response capacity?
