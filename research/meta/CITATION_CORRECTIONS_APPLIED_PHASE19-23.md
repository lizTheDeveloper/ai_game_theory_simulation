## Key Findings

### Fabrication Pattern #5: Author Name Substitution

The Phases 19-23 sweep uncovered **3 new author fabrications**, bringing total to 8. This represents a NEW error pattern:

**Pattern:**
1. **Paper exists** with correct DOI/title
2. **Authors completely substituted** with plausible-sounding names
3. **Details approximately correct** (year, journal, findings)
4. **Harder to detect** than HTTP 404 errors (paper is findable)

**Examples:**
- Dorn/Fuest/Potrafke → Polyakova/Kocks/Udalova/Finkelstein (0% author overlap)
- Clarke/Phelan/Kagoro/Azcona/Mensah → Ranger/Mahul/Monasterolo (0% overlap)
- "Xu, J." → Nature Editorial (unsigned - authors fabricated from thin air)
- "Hua, J." → Rivera et al. (0% overlap - all 6 names wrong)

**Why this is dangerous:**
- Creates false attribution trails in research
- Makes citation tracking impossible
- Harder to verify than obvious fabrications
- Could propagate through academic literature if uncorrected
