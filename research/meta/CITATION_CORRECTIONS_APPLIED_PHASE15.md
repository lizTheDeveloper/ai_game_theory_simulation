### AI Interpretability Evidence

The 5 Anthropic papers (2024-2025) provide robust empirical backing for interpretability interventions:

1. **Simple Probes (2024):** >99% AUROC - linear classifiers work
2. **Reasoning Models (April 2025):** <40% faithfulness - reasoning is opaque
3. **Alignment Faking (Dec 2024):** 7-24% baseline → 78% after RL - deception increases with training
4. **SHADE-Arena (June 2025):** 80% detection requires 20% false positive rate - tradeoff confirmed
5. **Persona Vectors (Aug 2025):** r=0.76-0.97 correlation - trait detection works
6. **Scaling Monosemanticity (2024):** Production SAEs on Claude 3 Sonnet - scales to real models

**Implication:** Ensemble approach (all 5 techniques) is necessary - no single method achieves high detection without unacceptable false positive rates.
