## System Overview

**AI Coordination & Transition Management** models how AI coordination quality and deployment pacing affect transition mortality when breakthrough technologies are deployed rapidly.

**Key Claims:**
1. God mode instant deployment: ~30% mortality (uncoordinated)
2. Moderate AI coordination (0.6): ~9-12% mortality
3. Optimal coordination (1.0): ~3-6% mortality
4. Coordination failures: 10-20% probability, 2-5× mortality spike
5. Rebound effects: 5-10% effectiveness decay per year

## Verification Protocol

For each citation:

1. **Find paper:** Use DOI, journal website, arXiv, Google Scholar
2. **Read abstract:** Does topic match claim?
3. **Search for claim:** Ctrl+F for keywords (e.g., "42%", "80%", "10-20%")
4. **Quote passage:** Extract exact text supporting claim
5. **Assess accuracy:**
   - ✅ **VERIFIED:** Paper directly supports claim
   - ⚠️ **PARTIAL:** Paper discusses topic but not exact value
   - ❌ **UNVERIFIED:** Paper doesn't support claim / claim extrapolated
6. **Document in verification log**
