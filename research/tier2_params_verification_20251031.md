#### Claim 1.2: USSR Collapse - Ukraine referendum

**Original Claim:**
> "1991: 76.4% vote for independence in Ukraine referendum"

**Verification Status:** 🚨 **CRITICAL ERROR - Reversed context**

**Evidence:**
> "A total of 113.5 million people, or 76.4% of USSR citizens voted **for** the preservation of the Soviet Union in the **March 1991** referendum"
> — Multiple sources (Wikipedia, CSCE)

> "An overwhelming majority of **92%** (or 92.3%) of voters approved the declaration of independence made by the Verkhovna Rada on August 24, 1991 [in the **December 1, 1991** referendum]"
> — Wikipedia: 1991 Ukrainian independence referendum

**Context Match:** ❌ **INVERTED MEANING**

**Issue:** The 76.4% figure refers to support **FOR preserving the USSR** (March 1991), not independence. The December 1991 independence referendum showed **92% support for independence**. This is a significant contextual error that reverses the claim's meaning.

**Correct Interpretation:** Ukraine voted 76.4% to preserve USSR in March, then 9 months later voted 92% for independence (December) - showing dramatic shift after August 1991 coup attempt.
