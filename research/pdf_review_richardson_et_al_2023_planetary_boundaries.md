## 📊 KEY PAPER CONTRIBUTIONS

### 1. New Functional Biosphere Integrity Metric: HANPP
**Human Appropriation of Net Primary Production (HANPP):**
- Holocene baseline: 55.9 Gt C/year (very stable)
- 2020 actual NPP: 65.8 Gt C/year (with land use reduction)
- 2020 HANPP: **30% of Holocene mean NPP** (16.8 Gt C/year)
- **Boundary:** <10% HANPP
- **High-risk zone:** >20% HANPP
- **Status:** DEEP IN HIGH-RISK ZONE

**This boundary was transgressed in the late 19th century** - a century before climate became evident (1980s).

### 2. Freshwater Change Boundary Completely Revised
**Previous approach:** Only blue water consumption (global totals)
- Previous boundary: 4,000 km³/year
- Current consumption: ~1,700 km³/year
- **Old conclusion:** Within safe operating space

**New approach:** Monthly deviations from preindustrial variability (both blue and green water)
- Blue water boundary: <10.2% of land area with deviations
- Green water boundary: <11.1% of land area with deviations
- **Current status:** 18.2% (blue), 15.8% (green)
- **New conclusion:** BOTH TRANSGRESSED (since 1905 for blue, 1929 for green)

### 3. Earth System Modeling of Boundary Interactions
**Key finding:** Respecting land system change boundary is critical for climate goals

**Scenario modeling (POEM) - Cumulative carbon by 2100:**
- **350 ppm + 85/50/85% forest:** +25 Gt C source (small, stable)
- **450 ppm + 60/30/60% forest:** +132 Gt C source (moderate deforestation impact)
- **550 ppm + 40/20/40% forest:** +145 Gt C source (severe combined impact)

**Implication:** Failure to respect land system change boundary jeopardizes Paris Agreement goals.
