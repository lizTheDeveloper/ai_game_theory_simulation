## Executive Summary

**VERIFICATION STATUS: ✅ CONFIRMED**

The claim is **accurate**. Ocean acidification was officially designated as the 7th breached planetary boundary in the "Planetary Health Check 2025" report published on **September 24, 2025** by the Planetary Boundaries Science Lab at the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (PIK) in collaboration with Stockholm Resilience Centre researchers.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. **Official Announcement Date**
- **Date:** September 24, 2025
- **Publication:** "Planetary Health Check 2025: A Scientific Assessment of the State of the Planet"
- **Primary Source:** Planetary Boundaries Science Lab, Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (PIK)

### 2. **Confirmation of 7th Boundary Status**
The report explicitly states that **7 of 9** planetary boundaries have now been breached:

**Breached Boundaries (7):**
1. Climate Change (previously breached)
2. Biosphere Integrity (previously breached)
3. Land System Change (previously breached)
4. Freshwater Use (previously breached)
5. Biogeochemical Flows (previously breached)
6. Novel Entities (previously breached)
7. **Ocean Acidification** ⭐ **(NEW in 2025)**

**Within Safe Zone (2):**
- Ozone Depletion
- Aerosol Loading

### 3. **Scientific Basis**

**pH Change:**
- Surface ocean pH has fallen by **0.1 units** since pre-industrial era
- This represents a **30-40% increase in acidity** (logarithmic scale)
- The boundary was defined as **10% reduction from pre-industrial aragonite saturation state**
- This threshold was **crossed by year 2000** across the entire surface ocean

**Habitat Loss:**
- **43% reduction** in suitable habitat for tropical/subtropical coral reefs
- Up to **61% reduction** for polar pteropods
- **13% reduction** for coastal bivalves

**Primary Cause:**
- Rising CO₂ emissions from fossil fuel combustion
- Combined with ocean warming and deoxygenation

## Uncertainties and Limitations

### 1. **Report Type**
This is an **institutional research report**, not a traditional peer-reviewed journal publication. However:
- PIK is a highly credible research institution (Leibniz Association member)
- Stockholm Resilience Centre originated the planetary boundaries framework
- The assessment synthesizes peer-reviewed science
- This is the **second annual** Planetary Health Check (established process)

### 2. **Boundary Definition Evolution**
The report notes that boundary definitions have been **revised** based on new research:
- Original boundary may have been too lenient
- Revised threshold of 10% reduction (vs. previous definitions)
- Acknowledgment that boundary was likely crossed **by 2000**, but officially recognized in 2025

### 3. **Distinction: Physical vs. Recognized**
Important nuance:
- **Physical transgression:** Likely occurred ~2000-2020
- **Official recognition:** September 2025 (Planetary Health Check 2025)
- The wiki statement refers to the **recognition date**, not the physical crossing date

### 4. **Geographic Heterogeneity**
The boundary assessment is for the **global surface ocean average**. Regional variations exist:
- Polar regions particularly affected
- Coastal upwelling zones may be more vulnerable
- Tropical coral reef regions showing severe impacts
