## Pattern Recognition

### Type 1: Complete Fabrication
- arXiv:2506.01438 (404 error)
- Seripally blog post (unverifiable)

### Type 2: Author Name Substitution
- Paper **exists** with correct DOI
- Authors **completely wrong** (0% overlap in most cases)
- Details approximately correct (year, journal, topic)
- **Hardest to detect** - requires DOI verification

### Type 3: Institutional Misattribution
- Anthropic "Shard Theory" (actually Pope & Turner, AI Alignment Forum)
- Wrong organization credited with research

## Verification Red Flags

🚨 **High Risk Indicators:**
- "et al." with single-letter first names (J., C., etc.)
- No DOI or arXiv ID provided
- Blog posts or Medium articles cited as research
- Institutional claims without author names
- Recent dates (2024-2025) with no verification link

⚠️ **Medium Risk:**
- Multiple authors with common surnames
- Papers from 2023-2024 without DOI
- "Unpublished" or "in press" claims
- Unsigned editorials cited as research articles

✅ **Verification Steps:**
1. **Always check DOI** - Resolve to authoritative source
2. **Verify author names** - Cross-check with publisher website
3. **arXiv papers** - Confirm paper exists at arxiv.org/abs/[ID]
4. **Google Scholar** - Search exact title + authors
5. **Multiple sources** - Verify from 2+ independent sources
