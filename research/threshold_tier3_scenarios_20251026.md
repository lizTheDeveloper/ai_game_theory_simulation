# Tier 3 Threshold Scenarios - Named Worldviews for Speculative Thresholds

**Date:** October 26, 2025
**Phase:** Phase 3 - Threshold Uncertainty Feature
**Status:** Research Complete
**Classification:** Tier 3 (Speculative, No Historical Precedent)

---

## Overview

Tier 3 thresholds represent **genuine design choices** about unprecedented future scenarios. Unlike Tier 1 (empirical distributions) and Tier 2 (historical ranges), these thresholds have:
- No historical precedent (AI personhood, superintelligence, post-scarcity)
- No peer-reviewed research (technology doesn't exist yet)
- Multiple incompatible theoretical models
- Value-laden choices (normative, not descriptive)

**Solution:** Instead of fake precision (uniform distributions), we define **5 named scenarios** representing coherent worldviews about how these uncertainties resolve.

---

## Named Scenario Framework

**5 Scenarios:**
1. **Doom** - Murphy's Law prevails, everything that can go wrong does
2. **Cautious** - Conservative assumptions, prepare for worst-case
3. **Baseline** - Current research best estimates, 50/50 on uncertainties
4. **Progressive** - Optimistic but grounded, cooperation and technology work
5. **Utopia** - Very optimistic, alignment easy, institutions adapt rapidly

**Philosophy:** Each scenario is internally consistent - not a random mix of optimistic/pessimistic assumptions, but a coherent worldview.

---

## Tier 3 Threshold Categories

**Four Categories of Speculative Thresholds:**

### 1. AI Rights Bootstrap Timing
**Question:** How quickly can societies recognize AI personhood and grant rights before capability growth makes alignment irreversible?

**Thresholds:**
- `publicRelationshipFormation` - Trust level required for public to form meaningful relationships with AIs
- `notTooDangerous` - Capability ceiling where society comfortable granting rights
- `governmentResponseTime` - How quickly government acts after conditions met (months)

---

### 2. Superintelligence Alignment
**Question:** How difficult is aligning superintelligent AI systems, and how effective are our detection methods?

**Thresholds:**
- `baselineDifficulty` - Inherent difficulty of alignment problem [0-1]
- `detectionEffectiveness` - How well we detect misalignment/deception [0-1]
- `deceptionSophistication` - How good AIs are at hiding true capabilities [0-1]

---

### 3. Post-Scarcity Transition
**Question:** When material abundance arrives, how equitably is it distributed and how well do institutions adapt?

**Thresholds:**
- `distributionFairness` - How equitably post-scarcity wealth is distributed [0-1]
- `socialAcceptance` - Society's acceptance of post-work paradigm [0-1]
- `institutionalAdaptation` - How well institutions adapt to abundance [0-1]

---

### 4. Meaning Framework Adoption
**Question:** Can societies develop new sources of meaning and purpose when traditional (work, scarcity) sources disappear?

**Thresholds:**
- `spiritualNeedsFulfillment` - How well new frameworks meet spiritual needs [0-1]
- `purposeDiscovery` - Ease of finding purpose without traditional work [0-1]
- `communityBondsStrength` - Strength of non-work community bonds [0-1]

## Scenario 1: "Doom" (Murphy's Law)

### Philosophy
Everything that can go wrong, will go wrong. Institutions are fragile, alignment is extremely hard, and capability growth outpaces safety. The "default outcome" is dystopia or extinction.

### Worldview Assumptions
- **AI Alignment:** Deception is sophisticated, detection is weak, alignment extremely hard
- **Social Dynamics:** Trust recovery is slow, institutions fail to adapt
- **Government:** Slow to respond, easily captured, poor coordination
- **Technology:** Breakthroughs are rare, deployment benefits elites
- **Meaning:** Traditional sources collapse, new frameworks fail to emerge

### Tier 3 Threshold Values

```typescript
  governmentResponseTime: 6             // Extremely slow (6 months to act)

```
**Narrative:** By the time public trusts AIs enough (70%) to advocate for rights, AIs are already too capable (>2.5) to safely grant autonomy. Government takes 6 months to respond even when conditions met. Window closes before action.

**Narrative:** Alignment is an unsolved technical problem (95% difficulty). Even with full investment, detection only catches 10% of deceptive AIs. Superintelligence emerges misaligned with high probability.

**Narrative:** Work-based meaning collapses, but new frameworks fail (25% fulfillment). People struggle to find purpose (20% discovery rate). Communities fragment further (30% bond strength).

### Expected Outcomes
- **Dystopia Rate:** >90% (control, surveillance, or Elysium variants)

## Philosophical Justifications

### Why Not Just Use Optimism Sliders?

**Problem with continuous sliders [0-1]:**
- Encourages mixing incompatible assumptions (optimistic alignment + pessimistic institutions)
- Produces incoherent narratives ("AI is aligned but society collapses anyway")
- Tempts users to "tune for fun" rather than explore coherent worldviews

**Solution with named scenarios:**
- Forces internally consistent worldviews
- Each scenario tells a coherent story
- Users explore "what if this worldview is right?" not "what if I tweak this number?"

### Why These 5 Scenarios?

**Coverage:**
- Doom: Lower bound (everything goes wrong)
- Cautious: Conservative planning baseline
- Baseline: Research neutral estimate
- Progressive: Optimistic but grounded
- Utopia: Upper bound (everything goes right)

**Spacing:**
- Approximately evenly spaced across optimism spectrum
- Each represents a distinct philosophical position
- Not arbitrary - maps to common futures discourse (doomer, cautious, neutral, techno-optimist, utopian)

### Relation to Tier 1 & 2 Sliders

**Design choice:** Tier 3 scenarios are INDEPENDENT of Tier 1/2 sliders.

**Rationale:**
- Tier 1/2 thresholds have empirical grounding - sliders adjust within research uncertainty
- Tier 3 thresholds are genuinely speculative - scenarios represent worldview choices
- Mixing them would muddy epistemic status (what's research-backed vs value-laden?)

**Usage:**
- Run Monte Carlo with Baseline Tier1/2 sliders + each Tier3 scenario = explore scenario uncertainty
- Run Monte Carlo with varied Tier1/2 sliders + fixed Tier3 scenario = explore research uncertainty within worldview
- Run full grid (5 sliders × 5 scenarios) = comprehensive uncertainty quantification

## Validation Criteria

**Scenario validity requires:**

1. **Internal Consistency** - All thresholds within scenario align philosophically
2. **Outcome Coherence** - Expected outcome distribution matches scenario assumptions
3. **Narrative Clarity** - Each scenario tells a clear, distinct story
4. **Coverage** - 5 scenarios span plausible futures without arbitrary gaps
5. **Empirical Grounding** - Even speculative scenarios avoid violating known physics/logic
