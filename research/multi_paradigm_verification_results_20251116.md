## Citation 1: Indigenous Wellbeing Framework

### Bibliographic Verification

**Source:** Sangha, K. K., Dinku, Y., Costanza, R., & Poelina, A. (2024). "A comprehensive analysis of well-being frameworks applied in Australia and their suitability for Indigenous peoples." *International Journal of Qualitative Studies on Health and Well-being*, 19(1), 2321646.

- ✅ Journal confirmed: International Journal of Qualitative Studies on Health and Well-being
- ✅ Volume/Issue: 19(1)
- ✅ DOI: 2321646
- ✅ Publication: 2024 (accessible via PMC)

### Claim Verification

#### CLAIM 1: Country as Foundational

**Original Claim:**
> "Country (land/environment) is foundational to Indigenous wellbeing, not peripheral"

**Verification Status:** ✅ VERIFIED

**Exact Quote from Paper (Section 3):**
> "Country is the central to the Indigenous world to which all other domains of wellbeing such as cultural, spiritual, physical, economic, and social worlds are attached"

**Source Location:** Section 3: "Well-being frameworks specifically focused on Indigenous peoples"

**Analysis:**
- ✅ The word "central" establishes foundational status
- ✅ Explicitly states other domains are "attached" to Country (dependency relationship)
- ⚠️ Minor note: Quote has grammatical quirk ("the central to") but meaning is clear

#### CLAIM 2: Seven Interconnected Domains

**Original Claim:**
> "Seven interconnected domains: family, community, Country-connection, self-determination, health, material wellbeing, subjective wellbeing"

**Verification Status:** ✅ VERIFIED (with attribution note)

**Exact List from Paper (Section 3, citing Yap & Yu 2016):**
1. Strong family
2. Strong community
3. Connection to culture, Country, and identity
4. Self-determination
5. Health
6. Material wellbeing
7. Subjective wellbeing

**Source Location:** Section 3, Yawuru Mabu Liyan framework

**Analysis:**
- ✅ Seven domains confirmed
- ✅ List matches research file (minor wording differences: "strong family" vs "family")
- ✅ Attribution correct: Yawuru framework (Yap & Yu 2016)
- ⚠️ Note: Domain 3 is "Connection to culture, Country, and identity" (not just "Country-connection")

#### CLAIM 3: Liyan Concept

**Original Claim:**
> "Liyan concept (Yawuru): holistic self-Country-community connection"

**Verification Status:** ✅ VERIFIED

**Exact Quote from Paper (Section 3):**
> "wellbeing [mabu liyan – meaning a good life] is about feeling, being, doing and relating, and described as 'Liyan', which includes how people feel with themselves, how they feel regarding their connections to Country, and how they feel connected to family and community"

**Source Location:** Section 3

**Analysis:**
- ✅ Liyan concept confirmed
- ✅ Yawuru attribution confirmed (mabu liyan)
- ✅ Three-part structure: self + Country + family/community
- ✅ "Holistic" characterization is accurate (feeling, being, doing, relating)

#### Additional Supporting Quote

**Quote (Section 3):**
> "A pivotal part of Indigenous culture, community and social networks is Country that influences both individual and societal well-being"

**Significance:** Reinforces Country as foundational infrastructure for wellbeing systems.

## Citation 2: Global Democracy Metrics

### Bibliographic Verification

**Source:** V-Dem Institute (2025). *Democracy Report 2025: 25 Years of Autocratization – Democracy Trumped?*

**URL:** https://www.v-dem.net/publications/democracy-reports/

**Status:** ✅ VERIFIED
- ✅ Report published March 2025
- ✅ Title confirmed: "DR25: 25 Years of Autocratization – Democracy Trumped?"
- ✅ V-Dem Institute authorship confirmed
- ✅ Available in English, Portuguese, Spanish PDFs

### Claim Verification

#### CLAIM 1: Autocracies Outnumber Democracies (91 vs 88)

**Original Claim:**
> "Autocracies outnumber democracies for first time in 20 years (91 vs 88)"

**Verification Status:** ✅ VERIFIED

**Sources:**
1. **Democracy Without Borders** (March 2025): "For the first time in over 20 years, the number of autocracies surpasses the number of democracies"
2. **Multiple news outlets**: "The world now has 88 democracies (liberal and electoral) and 91 autocracies (electoral and closed)"

**Breakdown:**
- 91 autocracies = 56 electoral autocracies + 35 closed autocracies
- 88 democracies = 29 liberal democracies + 59 electoral democracies

**Analysis:**
- ✅ Numbers confirmed: 91 vs 88
- ✅ "First time in 20 years" confirmed
- ✅ Represents reversal from 2023 (when democracies outnumbered autocracies)

#### CLAIM 2: Liberal Democracies at 50-Year Low (12%)

**Original Claim:**
> "Only 12% of global population lives in liberal democracies (50-year low)"

**Verification Status:** ✅ VERIFIED

**Source:** V-Dem Democracy Report 2025 (via Democracy Without Borders)
> "The proportion of the world's population living in a liberal democracy is now the lowest it has been in 50 years – less than 12% of people worldwide"

**Analysis:**
- ✅ 12% figure confirmed (actually "less than 12%")
- ✅ 50-year low confirmed
- ⚠️ Note: Report says "less than 12%" (research file rounds to 12%)

#### CLAIM 3: 72% Under Autocratic Rule (5.8B people)

**Original Claim:**
> "72% (5.8 billion people) under autocratic rule"

**Verification Status:** ✅ VERIFIED

**Sources:**
1. **Ara.cat** (March 2025): "Democracy in decline: 72% of the world's population lives in autocracies"
2. **Web search results**: "There are 72% of people living under autocracies in 2024, up from 71% in 2023"

**Analysis:**
- ✅ 72% figure confirmed
- ✅ Math checks: 72% of ~8.0B global population = ~5.76B ≈ 5.8B
- ⚠️ Note: Some sources cite this as 2024 data (report published March 2025 covering 2024)

#### Additional Findings

**Countries that descended in 2024:**
- Belarus, Gabon, Lebanon, Niger descended from electoral to closed autocracies

**Democracy levels:**
- "The level of democracy enjoyed by the average person in the world in 2024 is down to 1985 levels"

**Autocratization wave:**
- 45 countries autocratizing (mentioned in research file, not yet verified in this search)
- Freedom of expression at 25-year low (mentioned in research file, not yet verified)
