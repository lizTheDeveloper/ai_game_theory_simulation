## Verification Methodology

### Layer 1: Citation Existence
For each source:
1. Verify URL is accessible (not 404, not paywalled beyond abstract)
2. Confirm author names, publication year, title match
3. Check publication venue is legitimate (not predatory journal)
4. Verify date of publication (must be 2024-2025 for "current" data)

### Layer 2: Claim Verification (CRITICAL)
For each claim:
1. **Locate specific passage** in source that supports the claim
2. **Quote verbatim** the relevant text from the source
3. **Compare claim to source** - does the source actually say this?
4. **Check for extrapolation** - is the claim within the source's scope?
5. **Identify methodology** - how was the figure derived?
6. **Mark as**:
   - ✅ **VERIFIED** - Source explicitly supports claim with quoted evidence
   - ⚠️ **PARTIAL** - Source supports general idea but not specific figure
   - ❌ **UNVERIFIED** - Claim not found in source or contradicted
   - 🔍 **NEEDS CLARIFICATION** - Ambiguous or requires additional context

### Common Issues to Watch For
- **Value extrapolation**: Paper discusses topic but doesn't provide the specific value cited
- **Temporal mismatch**: 2024 data attributed to 2020 publication
- **Misinterpretation**: Correlation stated as causation, estimates stated as confirmed
- **Cherry-picking**: Value cited without context of range or uncertainty
- **Phantom attribution**: Calculation performed by researcher but attributed to source
