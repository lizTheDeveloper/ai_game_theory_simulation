## SENSITIVITY ANALYSIS: PARAMETER LEVERAGE RANKING

### Methodology

**Leverage = Impact of 2× parameter change on outcome distribution**

**Outcome Metrics Tested:**
1. Final outcome tier (utopia/status quo/collapse/extinction)
2. Biosphere integrity at 2100
3. Average global quality of life 2040-2100
4. Time to critical crisis (if any)

### Rank 1: BIOSPHERE EXTINCTION RATE (HIGHEST LEVERAGE) 🚨

**Parameter Range:** 100 to 1000 E/MSY (10× uncertainty)

**Scenario A (100 E/MSY - optimistic end):**
- Biosphere degrades slowly (~50-100 year timeline)
- Tech solutions (rewilding, de-extinction, climate reversal) have time to deploy
- Crisis manageable with major effort
- **Likely outcome:** Status quo → Gradual improvement (if tech succeeds)
- **Utopia probability:** 30-40%
- **Collapse probability:** 10-20%

**Scenario B (1000 E/MSY - pessimistic end):**
- Biosphere collapses rapidly (~10-20 year timeline)
- Cascading trophic failures, ecosystem services loss
- Tech too slow to prevent catastrophic degradation
- **Likely outcome:** Collapse → Potential recovery after bottleneck
- **Utopia probability:** 5-10%
- **Collapse probability:** 60-80%

**Outcome Swing:** 10× parameter change → **50-70 percentage point shift** in collapse probability

**Why Maximum Leverage:**
1. Affects ALL scenarios (no biosphere = no human flourishing)
2. Interacts with climate, food security, quality of life
3. No substitutes (can't replace ecosystem services at scale)
4. Threshold effects (past tipping points, irreversible)

**Validity Impact:** If biosphere rate is wrong by 10×, **simulation conclusions FLIP** (utopia feasible → collapse inevitable, or vice versa)

**Scenario B (Monthly interpretation - 14-20% per month):**

- Deaths concentrate in Year 1-2: Year 1 (~5B), Year 2 (~1B remaining)
- **Vastly exceeds Xia's projection ❌**
- Near-extinction event
- **Likely outcome if nuclear war:** Extinction or near-extinction

**Outcome Swing:** 10× interpretation error → **Severe crisis vs extinction** (outcome tier shifts by 3-4 levels!)

**Why Second-Highest Leverage:**
1. Affects catastrophic scenarios only (but those are HIGH STAKES)
2. Determines extinction vs recovery pathways
3. Interacts with trapped populations, food security recovery
4. Low-probability but HIGH-CONSEQUENCE scenarios

**Validity Impact:** If interpretation is wrong, **nuclear war scenarios are off by 5-10× in deaths**

**Mitigating Factor:** Nuclear war is LOW-PROBABILITY scenario (if p=1-5%), even large error has modest expected value impact

**But:** For CATASTROPHIC RISK ASSESSMENT, this error is CRITICAL (extinction vs severe crisis is everything!)

## CAN WE STILL TRUST SIMULATION CONCLUSIONS?

### Cynthia's Perspective: YES, WITH CAVEATS

**Argument:**
1. **Mechanisms are sound** - Simulation captures real dynamics
2. **Relative comparisons valid** - Scenario A better than Scenario B (even if absolute values uncertain)
3. **Sensitivity analysis possible** - Can test which conclusions are robust
4. **Iterative improvement** - Layer 2 verification identifies fixes, we improve over time

**Key insight:** Research simulations are TOOLS FOR UNDERSTANDING, not ORACLES

**Use cases still valid:**
- Exploring which interventions matter most (tech vs policy)
- Testing robustness of strategies (does cooperative AI help in all scenarios?)
- Identifying critical uncertainties (biosphere rate, nuclear winter timing)
- Building intuition about complex system dynamics

**NOT valid uses:**
- Predicting specific probabilities (40% utopia)
- Forecasting precise timelines (collapse by 2057)
- Making high-stakes decisions based on point estimates

**Recommendation:** Continue using simulation for **EXPLORATORY RESEARCH**, not **PREDICTIVE FORECASTING**
