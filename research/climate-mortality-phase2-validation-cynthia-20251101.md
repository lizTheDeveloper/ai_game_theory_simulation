### 5. Keystone Species Cascade Multiplier (2.5×)

**Source:** Derived from Joshua Tree example (43% bird diversity decline)

**Claimed Values:**
- Research file: "Keystone species multiplier = 2.5" (affects 2.5× other species)
- Specification: Uses 2.5× cascade multiplier

**Validation:**
⚠️ **REASONABLE INFERENCE** from Joshua Tree data

**Justification:**
- Joshua Tree loss → 43% bird species decline
- If Joshua Tree represents ~17% of tree species, then 43/17 ≈ 2.5
- Mechanism: Keystone species provide habitat/food for dependent species

**Concerns:**
⚠️ 2.5× is inferred, not directly stated in research
⚠️ May vary by ecosystem (Joshua Tree may not be representative)

**Mitigation:**
- Conservative estimate (some keystone species have larger cascades)
- Sensitivity analysis can test 1.5-4.0× range
- Mechanism is well-established in ecology

**Confidence:** MEDIUM-HIGH - Plausible inference from solid data
