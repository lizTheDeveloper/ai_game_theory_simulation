## Synthesis: Tiered Augmentation Spectrum

**Recommended Implementation:** Three-tier system within expanded bionicSkills.ts

### TIER 1: AI-Assisted Skills (TRL 7-8) - VALIDATED
**Source:** Existing bionicSkills.ts + research backing
**Status:** Deploy immediately with revisions

**Technologies:**
- AI coding assistants (Copilot, Cursor, Aider)
- AI tutors and education tools (Khan Academy, Duolingo AI)
- Productivity amplification (ChatGPT, Claude, workflow automation)
- Creative tools (Midjourney, Sora, DALL-E)

**Mechanics:**
- Differential benefits: novices +60%, experts +20% (Peng et al. 2023)
- Skill erosion over time (METR "deprioritization" 2025)
- Digital divide by access (65.1pp productivity-wage gap, Acemoglu 2024)
- Segment-level tracking (port from humanEnhancement.ts)

**Research Citations:**
- Peng et al. 2023 (GitHub Copilot novice vs expert gains)
- Noy & Zhang 2023 (consultant productivity convergence)
- METR 2025 (deprioritization safety concern)
- Acemoglu et al. 2024 (productivity-wage decoupling)

### TIER 2: Physical/Sensory Augmentation (TRL 3-7) - EMERGING
**Source:** New implementation based on Visionary + Skeptic recommendations
**Status:** Phase 2 expansion (12-20h effort)

**Technologies:**
- **AR/VR workplace:** Apple Vision Pro, Meta Quest Pro, spatial computing
- **Wearable sensors:** Meta EMG wristbands, smartwatches, fitness trackers
- **Neuromodulation:** Transcranial stimulation (focus enhancement), consumer EEG headbands
- **Exoskeletons:** Industrial (Sarcos, Ekso), medical (ReWalk), consumer experiments
- **Brain-sensing:** Neurable (AR eye-tracking + EEG), Muse (meditation/focus)

**Mechanics to Model:**
- **Cost barriers:** $3,499 Vision Pro vs $299 Meta Quest vs $0 smartphone AR
- **Workplace adoption:** Tech sector vs manufacturing vs service (differential access)
- **Productivity gains:** +10-30% for compatible tasks (AR assembly, VR training)
- **Physical enhancement:** +20-50% endurance for exoskeletons (industrial workers)
- **Cognitive enhancement:** +5-15% focus/attention (neuromodulation, limited evidence)

**Augmentation Divide Scenarios:**
- **Elites:** Full stack (Vision Pro + wearables + neuromodulation) = compound advantage
- **Middle class:** Partial access (smartphone AR + basic wearables)
- **Underserved:** No access → expanding gap

**Research Citations:**
- Billinghurst et al. 2015 (AR productivity gains in assembly tasks)
- De Looze et al. 2016 (exoskeleton endurance gains, industrial applications)
- Coffman et al. 2014 (transcranial stimulation cognitive effects, mixed evidence)
- Liberati et al. 2018 (consumer EEG reliability concerns)

**Implementation Notes:**
- Model adoption curves by income/sector (tech fast, manufacturing slow)
- Track compound effects (multiple augmentations > sum of parts)
- Include backlash scenarios (privacy concerns, workplace monitoring via wearables)

### TIER 3: Speculative Enhancement (TRL 0-2) - REMOVE
**Source:** Existing humanEnhancement.ts (portions to delete)
**Status:** Out of scope for 2025-2045 simulation

**Technologies to REMOVE:**
- Brain-computer interfaces (invasive, Neuralink-style)
- Consciousness uploading
- Human-AI merger pathways
- Genetic intelligence enhancement
- Radical life extension (longevity escape velocity)

**Rationale (all agents agree):**
- Too speculative (TRL 0-2, no validated deployment)
- Timeline beyond simulation scope (2045+ at earliest)
- High uncertainty makes modeling unreliable
- Better served by separate speculative analysis tools

## Key Decisions & Rationale

### Decision 1: Include TRL 3-7 "Middle Ground"
**Rationale:** Skeptic and Visionary both identified this as critical gap. AR/VR, wearables, and exoskeletons are deploying NOW and creating augmentation divide in 2025, not 2040s.

**Impact:** Increases scope from 8-12h → 24-36h, but models realistic near-term inequality.

### Decision 2: Downgrade TRL 8-9 → TRL 7-8
**Rationale:** Skeptic correctly noted AI tools are in pilot deployment (TRL 7) or early operational use (TRL 8), not fully proven (TRL 9).

**Impact:** More accurate uncertainty modeling, aligns with research standards.

### Decision 3: Remove All TRL 0-2 Speculation
**Rationale:** All three agents agree. BCIs, uploading, merger are too speculative for 2025-2045 simulation timeframe.

**Impact:** Cleaner, more defensible model focused on validated/emerging tech.

### Decision 4: Two-Phase Implementation
**Rationale:** Allows incremental validation. Phase 1 consolidates existing work, Phase 2 expands to middle ground.

**Impact:** Lower risk, can stop after Phase 1 if needed (though Phase 2 strongly recommended).
