**ADJUSTED CLAIM:** Base chaotic mortality = 3.5% annual (economic disruption only, excluding political violence)
**CONFIDENCE:** MEDIUM-HIGH (historical data, conservative adjustment)
**STATUS:** ⚠️ ADJUSTED (5.5% → 3.5%)

#### 2.2 AI Coordination Effectiveness

**CLAIM (Original):** AI coordination can achieve 92-95% mortality reduction vs. chaotic deployment
**SOURCE:** research/transition_mortality_coordination_effectiveness_20251115.md (Marshall Plan extrapolation)
**FILE LOCATION:** plans/coordinated_technology_deployment.md:89

#### 2.3 Cash Transfer / UBI Effectiveness

**CLAIM:** $1,000 cash transfer → 10-20% mortality hazard reduction
**SOURCE:** Behrman et al. (2011) Social Security data
**FILE LOCATION:** research/transition_mortality_coordination_effectiveness_20251115.md:225

**VERIFICATION FINDINGS (Sylvia):**
- ⚠️ **GENERALIZATION ISSUE:** Elderly-specific finding (Social Security) applied to working-age populations
- ⚠️ **CONTRADICTION:** Meta-review (Banerjee et al. 2024 AER) finds minimal mortality impact from UBI trials
- **QUOTE FROM CONTRADICTORY PAPER:**
  > "No meaningful improvements in child development, education, or health, along with consistent reductions in labor force participation and earnings."
- 🔬 **RESOLUTION:**
  - Social Security (elderly, permanent) ≠ UBI (working-age, temporary trials)
  - Elderly mortality more sensitive to income (healthcare costs, fixed income vulnerability)
  - Working-age mortality less sensitive (labor market substitution)

**ADJUSTED CLAIM:** UBI mortality reduction = 25% effectiveness (down from 40% in original)
**CONFIDENCE:** MEDIUM (elderly data exists, working-age extrapolation uncertain)
**STATUS:** ⚠️ ADJUSTED (40% → 25% support system weight)

## Functional Form Validation

**PROPOSED MODEL (Original):**
```typescript
expectedMortality = chaoticBaseline *
  (1 - coordinationQuality * 0.95) *
  (deploymentSpeed ^ 1.3) *
  (1 - supportCoverage * 0.80) *
  (1 - regionalAdaptation * 0.60)
```

**VERIFICATION FINDINGS (Sylvia):**
- ❌ **FORM MISMATCH:** Research says "additive with diminishing returns", model uses multiplicative
- ⚠️ **EDGE CASE ISSUE:** Perfect coordination (all params = 1) → 0.022% mortality (unrealistically low)
- 🔬 **RECOMMENDED FORM:**

```typescript
// Additive model with diminishing returns (per Sylvia's recommendation)
const baseMortality = BASE_CHAOTIC_MORTALITY; // 3.5% annual
const coordinationReduction = coordinationEffectiveness * 0.60; // Max 60% reduction
const supportReduction = supportQuality * 0.40; // Max 40% reduction

// Cap total reduction at 95% (minimum 0.5% mortality even with perfect systems)
const totalReduction = Math.min(0.95, coordinationReduction + supportReduction);

// Net mortality (floor at 0.001 = 0.1%)
const annualMortality = Math.max(0.001, baseMortality * (1 - totalReduction));
```

**ADJUSTED MODEL:**
- Base chaotic mortality: 3.5% (down from 5.5%)
- AI coordination: 50-75% effectiveness (down from 92-95%)
- Support systems: Additive (not multiplicative)
- Maximum reduction: 95% cap (minimum 0.5% mortality floor)

**CONFIDENCE:** HIGH (conservative bounds, fail-safe minimums)
**STATUS:** ⚠️ ADJUSTED (multiplicative → additive with caps)
