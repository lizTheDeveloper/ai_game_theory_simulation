### Metadata Error Patterns (6 total)
1. **Date inflation:** Citations dated 1-3 years later than publication
   - Mosleh 2024 → 2021 (3-year error)
   - Richardson 2024 → 2023 (1-year error)
   - Brady 2020 → 2019 (1-year error)
2. **Lead author misattribution:** Co-author promoted to lead (Hickel vs Fanning/Raworth)
3. **Volume/page errors:** Incorrect journal metadata (Brady, Hickel)

### High-Risk Indicators
- ⚠️ "et al." without full author list
- ⚠️ Single-letter first names (J., C., etc.)
- ⚠️ Institutional claims without verification
- ⚠️ Blog posts masquerading as academic papers
- ⚠️ Unsigned editorials cited as research articles

### Quality Issues
- Missing arXiv IDs
- Incorrect paper titles
- Wrong publication dates
- Missing DOIs
- Incomplete author lists
