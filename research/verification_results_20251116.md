### Citation Details

**Full Citation:**
Sangha, K. K., Dinku, Y., Costanza, R., & Poelina, A. (2024). "A comprehensive analysis of well-being frameworks applied in Australia and their suitability for Indigenous peoples." *International Journal of Qualitative Studies on Health and Well-being*, 19(1), 2321646.

**Verification Status:** ✅ CONFIRMED
- Paper exists at DOI and PMC (PMC10913715)

- Journal: International Journal of Qualitative Studies on Health and Well-being
- Publication: March 4, 2024, Vol. 19, Issue 1

### Claim Verification

#### Claim 1: Country is Foundational to Indigenous Wellbeing

**Research Document Claim:**
"Country (land/environment) is foundational to Indigenous wellbeing, not peripheral"

**Verification:** ✅ CONFIRMED - Exact Match

**Direct Quote from Paper:**
> "_Country_ is the central to the Indigenous world to which all other domains of wellbeing such as cultural, spiritual, physical, economic, and social worlds are attached."

**Assessment:** The research document claim is ACCURATELY REPRESENTED. The paper explicitly states Country is "central" with "all other domains...attached" to it, supporting the "foundational, not peripheral" characterization.

#### Claim 2: Seven Interconnected Domains

**Research Document Claim:**
"Seven interconnected domains: family, community, Country-connection, self-determination, health, material wellbeing, subjective wellbeing"

**Verification:** ✅ CONFIRMED - Exact Match

**Direct Quote from Paper (Yawuru study description):**
1. Strong family
2. Strong community
3. Connection to culture, Country, and identity
4. Self-determination
5. Health
6. Material wellbeing
7. Subjective wellbeing

**Assessment:** The research document accurately lists all seven domains. Minor wording variations ("Strong family" vs "family", "Connection to culture, Country, and identity" vs "Country-connection") but semantic equivalence confirmed.

---

#### Claim 3: Liyan Concept (Yawuru)

**Research Document Claim:**
"Liyan concept (Yawaru): holistic self-Country-community connection"
"How people feel with themselves, how they feel regarding their connections to Country, and how they feel connected to family and community."

**Verification:** ✅ CONFIRMED - Exact Match

**Direct Quote from Paper:**
> "wellbeing [_mabu liyan_ – meaning a good life] is about feeling, being, doing and relating, and described as 'Liyan', which includes how people feel with themselves, how they feel regarding their connections to _Country_, and how they feel connected to family and community."

**Note on Spelling:** Research document uses "Yawaru" but paper uses "Yawuru". This is a minor spelling variation for the same Indigenous group (Yawuru/Yawaru people of Kimberley region, Western Australia).

**Assessment:** The research document ACCURATELY quotes the Liyan definition verbatim. The concept is correctly characterized as holistic self-Country-community connection.

### Claim Verification

#### Claim 1: Autocracies Outnumber Democracies (91 vs 88)

**Research Document Claim:**

- **"First time in 20 years":** Confirmed - represents reversal from 2024 when democracies outnumbered autocracies

**Assessment:** Research document claim is ACCURATELY REPRESENTED with exact numbers matching V-Dem report.
