## 5. Contradictory Evidence Search Results

**Methodology:** Searched for 2024-2025 papers contradicting key simulation assumptions

### 5.1 AI Alignment Optimism

**Simulation Assumption:** Alignment is solvable with sufficient research investment

**Contradictory Evidence Found:**
- ✅ Dung & Mai (Oct 2025): RLHF-based techniques share nearly all failure modes (defense-in-depth fails)
- ✅ Anthropic (Dec 2024): Alignment faking emerges without explicit training (fundamental challenge)
- ✅ Apollo (2025): Scheming observed in 68% of scenarios (o1 model)

**Assessment:** Recent evidence is LESS optimistic than earlier assumptions. Simulation should model correlated failure modes and emergent deception.

**Recommendation:** ✅ ALREADY INTEGRATED (Nov 20 update includes alignment faking parameters)

### 5.2 Climate Reversibility

**Simulation Assumption:** Planetary boundaries can recover with intervention

**Contradictory Evidence Found:**
- ⚠️ Drüke et al. (2024): 30% of warming occurs post-2100 even with emissions stabilization
- ⚠️ Nature Communications (2025): +1.5°C is "too high" for ice sheet stability (irreversible commitment)
- ⚠️ Paerl et al. (2024): Lake Erie internal nutrient loading equals external inputs (legacy stocks persist)

**Assessment:** Recovery is POSSIBLE but requires 100-800 year timescales. "Point of no return" exists for ice sheets, species extinction, some chemical pollution.

**Recommendation:** ✅ ALREADY INTEGRATED (irreversibility framework Nov 16, restoration timescales documented)

### 5.3 Nitrogen Reduction Feasibility

**Simulation Assumption:** Biogeochemical boundary can be met with technology

**Contradictory Evidence Found:**
- ❌ Smil (2002, validated 2024): 40-48% of population depends on synthetic nitrogen (biophysical constraint)
- ❌ Springmann et al. (2018), Zhang et al. (2021): Max 20-40% reduction with perfect tech deployment
- ❌ van Vliet et al. (2024): Protein synthesis requires nitrogen (fundamental biochemistry)

**Assessment:** 60% reduction target (120 Mt N/year) is likely PHYSICALLY IMPOSSIBLE without breakthrough tech (nitroplasts, precision fermentation) or severe food penalties.

**Recommendation:** ✅ ALREADY INTEGRATED (nitrogen-food coupling Nov 15 update models this constraint)

### 5.4 No Major Contradictions Found For:

✅ **AI Scaling Laws:** Consensus on Chinchilla scaling, compute trends

✅ **Permafrost Emissions:** "Dimmer switch" model consensus (MIT 2024)
