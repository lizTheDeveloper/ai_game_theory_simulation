## 2024-2025 AI-Enhanced Social Engineering Research

### AI-Generated Persuasion Effectiveness (Hou 2025)

**Citation**: Hou, Z. (2025). "Analyzing the persuasion mechanism of AI-generated rumors via the elaboration likelihood model." *Frontiers in Psychology*, 16, 1679853. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1679853

**Key Findings**:
- **90.5% peripheral route processing**: Users exposed to AI-generated content rely on emotional responses, not critical analysis
- **Only 9.5% central route engagement**: Minimal evidence-based evaluation or source questioning
- **Dataset**: 11,942 comments from Chinese platforms (Douyin, Bilibili, news) July 2024

**Mechanism - "Technological Realism"**:
> "The hyper-realistic quality of AI-generated content undermines users' capacity and willingness to engage in rigorous cognitive analysis, pushing them toward superficial decision-making pathways."

**Interpretation**: AI content exploits System 1 (intuitive) thinking over System 2 (deliberative) thinking, achieving persuasion through emotional manipulation rather than logical argument.

**Credibility**: Peer-reviewed Frontiers in Psychology, N=11,942, published 2025

### Cognitive Warfare: Russia-Ukraine Case Study (Paziuk et al. 2025)

**Citation**: Paziuk, A., Lande, D., Shnurko-Tabakova, E., & Kingston, P. (2025). "Decoding manipulative narratives in cognitive warfare: a case study of the Russia-Ukraine conflict." *Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence*, 2025. https://doi.org/10.3389/frai.2025.1566022

**Key Findings**:
- **Dataset**: 4,000 Russian websites + 3,000 Telegram channels (Nov 2022-Mar 2023, Jan-Dec 2024)
- **Method**: Semantic analysis of manipulative narratives targeting psychological vulnerabilities
- **Nuclear threat narratives**: Emotional manipulation through existential fear

**Mechanism**: Cognitive warfare exploits audience susceptibilities through targeted narrative framing, using computational propaganda to identify and amplify psychological vulnerabilities.

**Credibility**: Peer-reviewed Frontiers in AI, multi-year dataset covering active conflict

### 2024-2025 Empirical Security Training Data

**Sources**: Brightside AI (2025), Spacelift (2025), Sprinto (2025), Keepnet Labs (2025), Hoxhunt (2025)

#### Generic Mass Phishing
- **Untrained employees**: 30-35% click rate (baseline)
- **After basic training**: 12-15% click rate (57% reduction)
- **Recognition rate**: Only 20% successfully recognize and report phishing

#### Targeted Spear Phishing (Human-Crafted)
- **Untrained employees**: 50-60% success rate
- **Trained employees**: 20-25% success rate (58% reduction)

#### AI-Powered Spear Phishing (2024-2025 Data)
- **Controlled testing (late 2024)**: **54% success rate** against mixed training levels
- **Effectiveness trend**: 31% less effective than human (2023) → **24% more effective** (March 2025)
- **Improvement rate**: AI attacks improving ~2.75x per year

#### Stubborn Resistance Threshold
- **Median persistent click rate**: **1.5%** even after repeated training
- **Phishing test failure**: 45% of employees fail simulations
- **Human factor in breaches**: 60% of all breaches involve human actions (Verizon DBIR 2025)

**Key Insight**: Security awareness training reduces phishing susceptibility by **up to 86% from baseline**, but a stubborn **1.5% floor** persists even with intensive training.

**Interpretation**: When targets **know it's a threat** and are **trained to resist**, human-crafted attacks drop to **1.5-3.2%**, but AI-enhanced attacks achieve **54%** by exploiting emotional/cognitive vulnerabilities.
