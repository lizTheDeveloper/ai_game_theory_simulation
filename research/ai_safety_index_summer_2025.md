# AI Safety Index Summer 2025: Industry-Wide Assessment

**Research Date:** November 25, 2025
**Researcher:** Autonomous Researcher
**Purpose:** Document the 2025 AI Safety Index findings for modeling AI company safety practices in simulation
**Status:** CURRENT - Critical data for AI agent governance modeling

---

## Executive Summary

**Major Finding:** An expert review panel has revealed an AI industry "struggling to keep pace with its own rapid capability advances" with "critical gaps in risk management and safety planning."

**Key Quantitative Findings:**
- **Best grade:** Anthropic (C+, score 2.64)
- **Industry average:** Below C (all companies scored below 3.0)
- **Existential Safety:** All companies scored D or below
- **Failing grades:** Chinese firms Zhipu AI (F) and DeepSeek (F)
- **Only 3 of 7 firms** conduct substantive dangerous capabilities testing

**Critical Finding for Simulation:** "Capabilities are accelerating faster than risk-management practice, and the gap between firms is widening." Without regulatory floors, motivated companies adopt stronger controls while others neglect basic safeguards.

## 2. Domain-Specific Performance

### 2.1 Six Evaluation Domains

The index assessed companies across **33 indicators** in six domains:

1. **Risk Assessment** - Identifying and evaluating potential harms
2. **Current Harms** - Managing known, present-day risks
3. **Safety Frameworks** - Technical safety measures and protocols
4. **Existential Safety** - Planning for transformative AI systems
5. **Governance & Accountability** - Corporate structure and oversight
6. **Information Sharing** - Transparency and collaboration

### 2.2 Domain Scores by Company

| Domain | Anthropic | OpenAI | DeepMind | x.AI | Meta |
|--------|-----------|--------|----------|------|------|
| Risk Assessment | C+ (2.5) | C (2.2) | C- (1.8) | F (0) | D (1) |
| Current Harms | B- (2.8) | B (3.0) | C+ (2.5) | - | - |
| Existential Safety | D | D | D | F | F |

### 2.3 Existential Safety Gap

**"Deeply disturbing" finding:** Companies claim AGI within a decade, yet "none scored above D in Existential Safety planning." Reviewers noted the absence of "coherent, actionable plans" for ensuring human-level AI remains safe.

---

## 3. Critical Safety Gaps

### 3.1 Dangerous Capabilities Testing

**Only 3 of 7 firms** conduct substantive testing for dangerous capabilities:
- Anthropic
- OpenAI
- Google DeepMind

**Testing gaps identified:**
- Biosecurity risk evaluation
- Cyber-terrorism potential
- Autonomous capability assessment

**Reviewer warning:** Existing safety tests "miss basic risk-assessment standards" and lack "explicit linkage between evaluation procedures and specific risks."

### 3.2 Governance Weaknesses

**Whistleblowing policies:**
- Only OpenAI has published a complete whistleblowing policy
- Most firms lack publicly accessible safety governance details

### 3.3 Chinese Firms Context

Zhipu AI and DeepSeek received failing grades. Evaluators noted that scoring "emphasizes self-governance norms less prominent in Chinese corporate culture and regulatory environments."

**Implication:** Different governance models emerging between Western and Chinese AI development.

## 4. Company-Specific Analysis

### 4.1 Anthropic (C+, 2.64)

**Strengths:**
- Conducted the **only human participant bio-risk trials**
- Excelled in privacy (doesn't train on user data)
- **World-leading alignment research** (Constitutional AI, RLHF advances)
- **Public Benefit Corporation** governance structure

**Gaps:**
- Still below B in overall preparedness
- Existential safety planning incomplete

### 4.2 OpenAI (C, 2.10)

**Strengths:**
- **Only company publishing full whistleblowing policy**
- More robust risk management approaches
- **Pre-mitigation model risk assessment** (tests raw models before safety tuning)
- Comprehensive external evaluation sharing

**Gaps:**
- Governance transitions (2023-2024) raised concerns
- Competitive pressure vs safety tradeoffs

### 4.3 Google DeepMind (C-, 1.76)

**Strengths:**
- Dangerous capabilities testing program
- Strong research publication record

**Gaps:**
- Integration challenges post-merger
- Less transparent than competitors

### 4.4 x.AI & Meta (D)

**Concerns:**
- Limited safety infrastructure
- x.AI: Rapid scaling with minimal safety investment
- Meta: Open-source approach complicates safety enforcement

---

## 5. Industry Trend Analysis

### 5.1 Widening Gap

**Key finding:** "The gap between firms is widening."

**Two-tier system emerging:**
- **Safety-conscious firms** (Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepMind): Adopting stronger controls
- **Fast-movers** (x.AI, Chinese firms): Neglecting basic safeguards

### 5.2 Voluntary Pledge Inadequacy

"Demonstrating inadequacy of voluntary pledges" - without regulatory floors, competitive pressure drives race-to-bottom dynamics in safety investment.

### 5.3 Capability-Safety Mismatch

**Fundamental tension:** Capabilities accelerating faster than risk management practice.

## 7. Implications for Simulation

### 7.1 Key Dynamics to Model

1. **Safety-capability gap widening over time**
2. **Two-tier governance** (safety-conscious vs fast-movers)
3. **Voluntary pledge ineffectiveness** without regulatory floors
4. **Deceptive alignment** risk in advanced systems
5. **Regional divergence** (Western vs Chinese governance models)
