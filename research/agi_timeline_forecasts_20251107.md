## Executive Summary

**Major finding:** AGI timeline forecasts have shortened dramatically from 2020-2025. Median expert predictions have compressed from ~50 years (2020) to 10-20 years (2025), with some industry leaders predicting arrival as early as 2026-2027.

**Key shifts:**
- **Metaculus community (2020):** Median 50 years away
- **Metaculus community (2025):** Median 2027-2031 (25% by 2027, 50% by 2031)
- **AI researchers (2022):** 50% probability by 2059
- **AI researchers (2023):** 50% probability by 2040 (19-year acceleration)

**Critical insight:** There is substantial disagreement between groups:
- **AI company leaders** (most optimistic): 25% chance by 2026
- **Metaculus forecasters:** 2027-2031
- **Published AI researchers:** ~2032-2040
- **Professional superforecasters:** 2029-2047
- **Academic consensus:** 2040-2050

**Definitional caveat:** Timelines vary significantly based on AGI definition. "Human-level performance on all tasks" vs "economically transformative AI" vs "superintelligence" yield different predictions.

## 4. Uncertainty and Disagreement

### 4.1 Definitional Variation

**Key finding (80,000 Hours):** "Forecasts varied significantly based on how experts defined AGI."

**Common definitions:**
1. **Human-level performance on all tasks** (most stringent)
2. **Economically transformative AI** (practical threshold)
3. **Superhuman performance on most cognitive tasks** (weaker threshold)
4. **Artificial general intelligence with self-improvement** (recursive improvement)

**Impact:** Definitional differences can shift timelines by 10-20 years.

---

### 4.2 Structural Uncertainty

**Sources of disagreement:**
1. **Scaling hypothesis validity** - Will current approaches reach AGI?
2. **Algorithmic breakthroughs** - Unknown unknown innovations
3. **Hardware constraints** - Physical limits vs engineering solutions
4. **Data bottlenecks** - Synthetic data quality and availability
5. **Safety/alignment delays** - Regulatory or technical slowdowns

---

### 4.3 Group Biases

**Industry leaders** (most optimistic): Incentives for hype, access to frontier capabilities, selection for optimism
**Academic researchers** (conservative): Peer review culture, skepticism of hype, slower information flow
**Professional forecasters** (moderate): Calibration incentives, base rate anchoring, external view
**Metaculus community** (volatile): Updates rapidly to new information, possible groupthink

## 5. Implications for Simulation Parameters

### 5.1 Baseline AGI Timeline Distribution

**Recommendation based on expert aggregation:**
- **10th percentile:** 2027 (optimistic industry leaders)
- **25th percentile:** 2030 (Metaculus, superforecasters)
- **Median (50th percentile):** 2035-2040 (academic consensus, adjusted industry)
- **75th percentile:** 2050 (conservative researchers)
- **90th percentile:** 2070 (long-tail scenarios, fundamental barriers)

**Justification:** Balanced between optimistic industry forecasts and conservative academic estimates, weighted toward recent expert surveys showing timeline compression.

---

### 5.2 Capability Growth Rate

**Pre-2024 baseline:** 4-5x compute per year
**2024-onward acceleration:** Coding capabilities doubling every 4 months (~8x per year on specific benchmarks)

**Critical insight:** General capabilities may scale slower than specific benchmarks. Coding/reasoning show fastest progress; robotic manipulation lags.

**Recommended parameter:**
- **Compute scaling:** 3-4x per year (conservative given constraints)
- **Effective capability scaling:** 2-3x per year (accounting for algorithmic improvements + compute)
- **Diminishing returns:** Model as power law with decreasing exponent over time

---

### 5.3 Timeline Compression Dynamics

**Observed phenomenon:** Expert timelines shortened by ~15-20 years from 2022 to 2023.

**Potential causes:**
1. **Faster-than-expected capability gains** (GPT-4, Claude 3.5, o1/o3 models)
2. **Availability bias** (recent impressive demos shift perception)
3. **Test-time compute breakthroughs** (o1 reasoning paradigm)
4. **Reduced skepticism** (transformers prove more capable than expected)

**Modeling approach:** Allow expert timelines to update based on observed capability trajectory in simulation.

---

### 5.4 Definitional Thresholds

**Recommendation:** Use multiple thresholds for different AGI definitions:

1. **Economically transformative AI:** When AI can automate >50% of cognitive work (likely 2-5 years before "full AGI")
2. **Human-level AGI:** Performance equal-or-better on 90% of human tasks
3. **Superintelligence:** Exceeds human experts in all domains

**Simulation implication:** "AGI arrival" should trigger different game mechanics depending on capability level, not binary on/off switch.

## 6. Critical Gaps and Limitations

### 6.1 Missing Research

**No empirical data on:**
- Superintelligent system behavior (by definition, doesn't exist yet)
- Recursive self-improvement dynamics
- AGI-level collective intelligence
- Phase transition speed from human-level to superintelligence

**Reliance on:** Theoretical arguments, expert intuition, analogies from narrow AI progress

---

### 6.2 Overconfidence Risk

**Dunning-Kruger at scale:** Experts making predictions about technology beyond current understanding may be systematically miscalibrated.

**Historical precedent:**
- **Nuclear fusion:** "20 years away" for 70+ years
- **Self-driving cars:** Overoptimistic timelines repeatedly adjusted
- **Human genome project:** Initially underestimated, then completed ahead of revised schedule

**Implication:** Median forecasts may still be overconfident. Consider wider uncertainty ranges.

---

### 6.3 Black Swan Events

**Unpredictable factors:**
- **Algorithmic breakthroughs** (e.g., transformers were not predicted in 2015)
- **Hardware innovations** (e.g., neuromorphic computing, quantum ML)
- **Data paradigm shifts** (e.g., effective synthetic data generation)
- **Regulatory shocks** (e.g., international compute governance)
- **Geopolitical disruption** (e.g., US-China AI decoupling, Taiwan semiconductor crisis)

**Modeling:** Include low-probability, high-impact events that shift timelines by ±10 years.

## 7. Recommended Simulation Parameters

### 7.1 AGI Arrival Distribution (Years from 2025)

```
Distribution: Lognormal
Median: 10-15 years (2035-2040)
10th percentile: 2 years (2027)
90th percentile: 45 years (2070)
Mean: ~18 years (accounting for long tail)
```

**Justification:** Matches expert aggregation, accounts for uncertainty, allows both near-term and long-tail scenarios.

---

### 7.2 Capability Scaling Function

```
Capability(t) = C_0 * (1 + growth_rate)^t * (1 - diminishing_factor * t)

Where:
- C_0 = initial capability level
- growth_rate = 1.5-2.5 (150-250% per year effective capability)
- diminishing_factor = 0.02-0.05 (2-5% reduction in growth rate per year)
- t = years since start
```

**Justification:** Captures observed acceleration in 2024 while modeling eventual slowdown from constraints.

---

### 7.3 Timeline Update Dynamics

**Model expert forecast compression:**
- If observed capability growth exceeds expectations → shorten AGI timeline by 10-20%
- If growth disappoints → extend timeline by 5-10%
- Update annually based on benchmark progress

**Rationale:** Mimics real-world expert updating behavior observed 2020-2025.
