## Key Findings

### Fabrication Pattern #4: Organizational Author Misattribution

The "Mosleh et al. (2024)" fabrication represents a NEW error pattern:
1. **Real study exists** (Martel & Rand 2024)
2. **Wrong author substituted** (Mosleh ≠ Martel)
3. **Wrong journal** (Psychological Science ≠ Nature Human Behaviour)
4. **Plausible surface details** (similar field, recent date)

**Why this is dangerous:**
- Harder to detect than HTTP 404 errors
- Author is real researcher in the field (Mosleh has other papers on misinformation)
- Statistics are approximately correct (27.6% vs 27%)
- Creates false attribution that could propagate through literature

### Journal Error Pattern

Both Christensen and Schumann & Klein had **wrong journals cited**:
- Christensen: Cyberpsychology → **First Monday** (completely different journal)
- Schumann & Klein: New Media & Society → **European Journal of Social Psychology** (different journal)

These are NOT typos - they're complete journal misattributions that affect citation tracking.

### Key Finding Attribution Error

The Mosleh (2021) paper had **completely wrong key findings** attributed to it:
- Cited findings were from Martel & Rand (2024) about warning labels being effective
- Actual findings are about debunking having PERVERSE effects (opposite direction)

This type of error is particularly dangerous because:
- The paper exists and is correctly cited in references
- But the research findings attributed to it are wrong
- Could lead to incorrect implementation of intervention mechanisms
