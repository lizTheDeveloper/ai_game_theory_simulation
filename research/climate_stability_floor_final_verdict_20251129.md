### ❌ Lenton et al. (2019) - "Climate tipping points — too risky to bet against"

**What code claimed:**
> "Self-limiting feedbacks prevent complete destabilization"

**What paper actually says:**
> "We argue that the intervention time left to prevent tipping could already have shrunk towards zero, whereas the reaction time to achieve net zero emissions is 20–30 years at best. Hence we might already have lost control of whether tipping happens. A **PLANETARY EMERGENCY** unfolding."

**Contradiction:** Paper warns about **LOSING CONTROL** and **PLANETARY EMERGENCY**, not self-limiting stability.

**Verdict:** ❌ **COMPLETE MISREPRESENTATION**

### ❌ Armstrong McKay et al. (2022) - "Exceeding 1.5°C global warming could trigger multiple climate tipping points"

**What code claimed:**
> "Not complete destabilization - Earth systems have resilience"

**What paper actually says:**
> "We provide a **global tipping points assessment**, proposing that nine global core tipping elements and seven regional impact tipping elements show signs of tipping point behavior... Many of these tipping points **could cascade into each other**, leading to an **AMPLIFYING DESTABILIZATION**."

**Contradiction:** Paper explicitly warns about **AMPLIFYING DESTABILIZATION** and **CASCADING TIPPING POINTS**, not resilience floors.

**Verdict:** ❌ **OPPOSITE OF WHAT CITED**

---

### ❌ Steffen et al. (2015) - "Planetary boundaries: Guiding human development on a changing planet"

**What code claimed:**
> "Earth remains habitable after boundary transgression"

**What paper actually says:**
> "Transgressing a boundary increases the risk that human activities could inadvertently drive the Earth System into a much less hospitable state, **damaging efforts to reduce poverty and leading to a DETERIORATION OF HUMAN WELL-BEING in many parts of the world, including wealthy countries**... Transgressing one or more planetary boundaries may be deleterious or even catastrophic due to the risk of **crossing thresholds that will trigger non-linear, ABRUPT ENVIRONMENTAL CHANGE** within continental- to planetary-scale systems."

**Contradiction:** Paper warns about **DETERIORATION**, **CATASTROPHIC RISK**, and **NON-LINEAR ABRUPT CHANGE**, not habitability preservation.

**Verdict:** ❌ **FUNDAMENTAL MISREADING**

---

### ⚠️ Zachos et al. (2008) - PETM Recovery

**What code claimed:**
> "Geological recovery demonstrates Earth system resilience"

**What paper actually says:**
> Recovery from PETM took **100-200 thousand years**. During this "recovery," the **"largest deep-sea mass extinction in 93 million years"** occurred.

**Problem:**
1. **Timescale mismatch:** 200ky recovery ≠ human-timescale resilience
2. **Mass extinction during recovery:** Not evidence of "resilience"
3. **Geological vs human timescales:** Irrelevant to civilization survival

**Verdict:** ⚠️ **TECHNICALLY CORRECT but MISLEADING** - Yes, Earth eventually stabilized, but on timescales and with casualties irrelevant to human civilization.

### Finding 1: Tipping Cascades Are Destabilizing (Wunderling et al. 2024)

**Source:** Wunderling, N., et al. (2024). "Climate tipping point interactions and cascades: a review." *Earth System Dynamics*, 15:41-74.

**Key Quote:**
> "We find indications that **MANY OF THE INTERACTIONS BETWEEN TIPPING ELEMENTS ARE DESTABILIZING**."

**On 1.5-2°C Warming:**
> "Tipping cascades **CANNOT BE RULED OUT** on centennial to millennial timescales at global warming levels between 1.5 and 2.0°C."

**Implications:**
- Most tipping interactions **accelerate** collapse, not limit it
- Cascades possible even within Paris Agreement targets
- Fast elements (AMOC, Amazon) can trigger rapid cascades at >2°C

**Verdict:** ❌ **ZERO SUPPORT FOR STABILITY FLOOR** - Shows opposite (destabilization)

### Finding 2: Net Feedbacks Becoming LESS Negative (2024-2025 Consensus)

**Source:** Multiple 2024-2025 climate feedback studies

**Key Finding:**
> "While the overall sum of feedbacks is negative, **IT IS BECOMING LESS NEGATIVE as greenhouse gas emissions continue**, meaning that warming is slower than it would be in the absence of feedbacks, but **WARMING WILL ACCELERATE** if emissions continue at current levels."

**Planck Feedback (Only Real Negative Feedback):**
- Stefan-Boltzmann radiation (T⁴) is fundamental physics - MORE heat radiated as Earth warms
- This is a **CONTINUOUS** dampening effect, NOT a stability "floor"
- Does NOT create minimum bound after tipping cascades
- Other positive feedbacks (methane, ice-albedo, cloud changes) can overwhelm Planck response

**Verdict:** ⚠️ **PARTIAL** - Planck feedback is real but does NOT support a 5% floor after cascades

## Why 5% Floor Is NOT Research-Backed

### 1. Confusion Between Continuous Feedback and Stability Floor

**Planck Feedback (Real):**
- Continuous dampening: As T↑ → radiation↑ (T⁴)
- Slows warming rate
- Operates at ALL temperatures

**5% Stability Floor (Not Real):**
- Claims minimum bound AFTER tipping cascades
- Implies Earth cannot go below 5% stability
- Suggests irreducible resilience

**These are DIFFERENT:**
- Continuous negative feedback ≠ Stability floor after collapse
- Slowing warming ≠ Preventing complete destabilization

### 2. Tipping Cascades Can Overwhelm Negative Feedbacks

**Wunderling et al. (2024) cascade examples:**
- Greenland ice loss → AMOC collapse → Amazon dieback
- Permafrost thaw → Methane release → Arctic amplification
- Ice loss → Albedo reduction → More warming → More ice loss

**NEW positive feedbacks** created by cascades can exceed Planck dampening.

### 3. Timescale Mismatch

**Geological recovery (Zachos 2008 PETM):**
- 100-200 thousand years to stabilize
- Largest mass extinction in 93Ma during "recovery"
- NOT human-timescale resilience

**Policy-relevant timescales (2025-2100):**
- Cascades possible on centennial scales (Wunderling 2024)
- Acceleration beyond 2°C (multiple studies)
- No 200ky available for civilization

### 4. Research Language Emphasizes Risk, NOT Stability

**2024-2025 papers say:**
- ❌ "Planet on the brink" (BioScience 2025)
- ❌ "Cascades cannot be ruled out" (Wunderling 2024)
- ❌ "Warming possibly accelerating" (State of Climate 2025)
- ❌ "Many interactions are destabilizing" (Wunderling 2024)
- ❌ "Planetary emergency" (Lenton 2019)

**They do NOT say:**
- ✅ "Self-limiting mechanisms preserve stability"
- ✅ "5% minimum stability floor"
- ✅ "Earth systems remain habitable after tipping"
