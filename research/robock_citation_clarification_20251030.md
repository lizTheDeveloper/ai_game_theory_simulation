## Attribution Best Practices

**When citing papers with multiple authors:**

1. **Use lead author** - Xia et al. 2022, not Robock et al. 2022
2. **Note co-authors if relevant** - "Xia et al. 2022 (with Robock as co-author)"
3. **Full citation in bibliography** - All authors listed
4. **Avoid informal shortcuts** - Even if Robock is more famous

**Exception:** If specifically discussing Robock's contribution to the field, can say "Robock's work on nuclear winter (e.g., Xia et al. 2022)"
