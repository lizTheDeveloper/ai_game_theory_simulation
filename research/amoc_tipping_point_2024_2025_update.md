## 1. van Westen et al. (2024) - Early Warning Signal

**Citation:** van Westen, R. M., Kliphuis, M., & Dijkstra, H. A. (2024). Physics-based early warning signal shows that AMOC is on tipping course. *Science Advances*, 10(6). https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adk1189

### Key Methodology

**Novel Early Warning Signal:** Minimum of AMOC-induced freshwater transport (Fov) at 34°S (southern boundary of Atlantic)

**Why this matters:**
- Previous studies lacked physics-based, observable early warning indicators
- Fov metric can be monitored in real-time using ocean observations
- Provides advance warning before actual collapse occurs

### Findings

**Tipping Point Timing:**
- **95% confidence interval:** 2025 to 2095
- **Median estimate:** Mid-century (2050s-2060s) under high emissions
- **Uncertainty:** ±35 years depending on freshwater forcing rates

**Current State Assessment:**
> "Reanalysis products indicate that the present-day AMOC is on route to tipping."

**Mechanism:**
1. Freshwater influx from Greenland ice melt increases
2. AMOC-induced southward freshwater transport (Fov) decreases
3. When Fov reaches minimum, system approaches tipping threshold
4. Beyond threshold, AMOC collapses within 50-150 years

### Critical Context

**This study represents a major shift in AMOC risk assessment:**
- Previous IPCC reports (AR5, AR6) assessed AMOC collapse as "low likelihood" this century
- New physics-based indicator suggests **higher probability** than previously thought
- Observable metric allows for ongoing monitoring and early detection

## 3. Climate Impacts of AMOC Collapse

**Sources:**
- [Ocean current 'collapse' could trigger 'profound cooling' in northern Europe – Carbon Brief](https://www.carbonbrief.org/ocean-current-collapse-could-trigger-profound-cooling-in-northern-europe-even-with-global-warming/)
- [The crucial tipping point scientists say could cause Atlantic Ocean collapse - Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/climate-environment/2024/02/09/atlantic-ocean-amoc-climate-change/)

### Regional Temperature Changes

**Northern Europe (Britain to Scandinavia):**
- **Winter temperature drop:** 10°C to 30°C within a century of collapse
- **London extreme winters:** Cold extremes approaching -20°C (one-in-10 winters)
- **Oslo extreme winters:** Plummeting to around -48°C
- **Sea surface temperature:** 10°C drop in northwest Europe

**Mechanism:**
> "Even with global warming, northern Europe could experience profound cooling if the AMOC collapses."

### Timeline of Impacts

**Transition timescale:** 50-150 years from collapse initiation to full shutdown
- **Fast regional feedbacks:** Few decades (salt transport between gyres)
- **Slower basin-wide feedbacks:** ~100 years (salt-advection feedback)

**Impact manifestation:** Decades to century after collapse begins
- **Cooling onset:** Within decades of collapse initiation
- **Full impact:** 50-100 years after collapse begins
- **Regional variability:** Faster impacts in northwest Europe, slower in Mediterranean

## 4. Counterpoint: Stabilization Mechanisms

**Citation:** Jackson, L. C., et al. (2024). Continued Atlantic overturning circulation even under climate extremes. *Nature*. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08544-0

### Alternative Perspective

**Key finding:** Southern Ocean wind-driven upwelling may stabilize AMOC

**Mechanism:**
- Increased Southern Ocean winds enhance upwelling
- Upwelling compensates for North Atlantic freshwater input
- AMOC weakens but doesn't fully collapse

**Implication for timing:**
> "Climate models suggest that the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation is unlikely to collapse this century, owing to stabilization from wind-driven upwelling in the Southern Ocean."

### Reconciling the Evidence

**Scientific consensus (as of 2024-2025):**
1. **AMOC is weakening:** High confidence (observational evidence)
2. **Tipping point exists:** High confidence (multiple models confirm)
3. **Timing of collapse:** Low-medium confidence (models disagree)
   - Pessimistic: 2025-2095 (van Westen et al.)
   - Optimistic: Beyond 2100 (stabilization scenarios)
4. **Impact severity if collapse occurs:** High confidence (regional cooling 10-30°C)

**Simulation approach:** Model both scenarios with probability distributions
- **Early collapse scenario:** 20-40% probability by 2075
- **Delayed collapse scenario:** 40-60% probability by 2100
- **Stabilization scenario:** 10-30% probability (weakening but no collapse)
