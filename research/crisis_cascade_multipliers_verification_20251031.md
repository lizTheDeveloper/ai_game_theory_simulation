### Citation Management

**How did these errors occur?**

Likely causes:
- Copy-paste errors from different papers
- Confusion between similar papers (e.g., Phelan on COVID-climate but NOT on compound risk multiplier)
- Inadequate verification of author lists before publication
- Mixing of sources without careful tracking

**Prevention strategies:**
- Always verify author lists against DOI/official publication page
- Use citation management software (Zotero, Mendeley) to prevent copy-paste errors
- Cross-check all citations against PubMed, Google Scholar, or journal websites
- When uncertain, include "[verify authors]" flag for later checking
