## Simulation Parameters Extracted

### Governance Event: International AI Moratorium Proposal

**Trigger Conditions:**
- AI capabilities approach ASI-relevant thresholds
- Expert risk assessments exceed baseline concern
- Catastrophic near-miss event occurs
- Coalition formation (US + China coordination)

**Moratorium Duration:**
- Minimum: Years (short-term pause)
- Maximum: Decades (until robust alignment solutions)
- Modal estimate: 10-20 years

**Compute Restrictions:**
- **Hard prohibition:** Training runs >10²⁴ FLOP
- **Post-training prohibition:** Runs >10²³ FLOP
- **Cluster threshold:** >16 H100-equivalents (~$500k, 990 TFLOP/s each)

**Enforcement Timeline:**
- **Day 1:** Consolidate >10,000 H100 clusters
- **Day 10:** Consolidate >1,000 H100 clusters
- **Day 100:** Consolidate >100 H100 clusters
- **Year 2:** All covered chip clusters under monitoring

**Monitoring Infrastructure Costs:**
- On-chip tamper-resistant mechanisms (per-chip cost)
- Satellite/aerial surveillance (capital + operational)
- Whistleblower programs (institutional overhead)
- International verification regime (diplomatic costs)

**Success Probability Factors:**
- US-China cooperation: LOW baseline probability
- Global compliance: Depends on coalition size
- Technical feasibility: On-chip monitoring unproven at scale
- Evasion risk: Smuggling, clandestine clusters, algorithmic efficiency improvements
