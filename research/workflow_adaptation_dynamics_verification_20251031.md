### SECTION 5: Threshold Effects & Network Effects

#### Claim 5.1: Innovation Diffusion Theory - Rogers

**Citation:** Innovation Diffusion Theory (Rogers, validated 2024 context)

**Claimed Statistics:**
- Critical mass: 15-25% adoption triggers network effects
- S-curve phases:
  - Innovators: 2.5%
  - Early adopters: 13.5%
  - Early majority: 34%
  - Late majority: 34%
  - Laggards: 16%
- Threshold for mainstream adoption: ~16% total

**Verification Status:** ⚠️ **PARTIALLY VERIFIED - Ranges vary**

**Source Information (Rogers Diffusion of Innovations Theory):**

**Critical Mass Range:**
> "After about 10-25% of system members adopt an innovation, there is relatively rapid adoption by the remaining members, marking a critical turning point."

> "When a new idea or behavior is adopted by 15 to 20 percent of an audience, it has the critical mass to spread on its own."

**S-Curve Pattern:**
> "Empirically, the successful spread of an innovation follows an S-shaped curve. If the cumulative number of adopters is plotted, the result will be an S-Shaped curve that starts slowly, accelerates until half of the individuals in system have adopted, and then slows down again."

**Adopter Categories (Rogers Framework):**
The specific percentages (2.5%, 13.5%, 34%, 34%, 16%) ARE from Rogers' theoretical framework:
- Innovators: 2.5%
- Early Adopters: 13.5%
- Early Majority: 34%
- Late Majority: 34%
- Laggards: 16%

**Assessment:**

✅ **Rogers framework percentages (2.5%, 13.5%, etc.) are CORRECT** - these are theoretical categories from Rogers' Diffusion of Innovations.

⚠️ **Critical mass range varies by source:**
- Research file claims: "15-25%"
- Literature shows: "10-25%" (some sources) or "15-20%" (other sources)

⚠️ **These are THEORETICAL categories, not empirically measured for AI adoption:**
The document applies Rogers' general framework to AI adoption, which is methodologically sound, but the specific percentages are theoretical idealization, not empirical measurements of actual AI adoption patterns.

**Validation in 2024 Context:**
The claim that this is "validated 2024 context" is PARTIALLY SUPPORTED:
- HBS 2024, EPOCH 2024, McKinsey 2024 research all show S-curve patterns
- 13% early adopters (McKinsey) aligns with Rogers' 13.5% early adopter category
- But exact Rogers percentages not empirically measured for AI specifically

**Credibility:** Framework is VALID and widely applied, but should be labeled as "theoretical framework applied to AI context" rather than "empirically measured for AI adoption."
