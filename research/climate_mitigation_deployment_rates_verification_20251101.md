### 2. **Carbon Budget Discrepancy: 275 GtCO₂ vs. 250 GtCO₂ (MEDIUM PRIORITY)**

**Issue:** Research document claims 275 GtCO₂ remaining for 1.5°C from January 2024, but Lamboll et al. (2023) reports 250 GtCO₂ from January 2023. After 2023 emissions (~40 GtCO₂), the January 2024 budget should be ~210 GtCO₂, not 275 GtCO₂.

**Impact:** Core parameter for simulation - carbon budget is foundational for net-zero timelines.

**Recommendation:** Clarify which carbon budget estimate is used (Global Carbon Budget 2023 vs. Lamboll et al. 2023 vs. IPCC AR6) and reconcile the 275 GtCO₂ figure. If using a different methodology or confidence interval, explain explicitly.
