Research requirements for Enhanced Biodiversity Modeling feature:

**Research needed (2024-2025 peer-reviewed sources):**

1. Food web collapse mechanics:
   - Trophic cascade dynamics
   - Energy transfer efficiency between trophic levels
   - Network effects in ecosystem collapse
   - Mass extinction timescales

2. Keystone species modeling:
   - Extinction threshold parameters
   - Species loss cascade propagation rates
   - Network centrality measures for ecosystems
   - Recovery timescales after keystone loss

3. Parameter values needed:
   - Trophic efficiency (% energy transfer per level)
   - Keystone species extinction thresholds
   - Cascade propagation rates (how fast extinctions spread)
   - Ecosystem resilience metrics
   - Recovery timescales (if possible)

4. Mechanism descriptions:
   - How species loss triggers cascades (mathematical models)
   - Trophic interaction modeling approaches
   - Network effects in food webs
   - Links to planetary boundaries (biosphere integrity)

5. Integration with existing systems:
   - Environmental debt accumulation
   - Quality of Life impacts (food security)
   - Climate change interactions
   - Pollution effects on food webs

**Output format:**
Save as research/biodiversity_food_webs_YYYYMMDD.md with citations, parameter extraction, mechanism descriptions, and integration notes.

**Quality requirements:**
- 2+ peer-reviewed sources per major parameter
- Extract specific numeric values where available
- Explain mechanisms, not just effects
- Map interactions with existing simulation systems
